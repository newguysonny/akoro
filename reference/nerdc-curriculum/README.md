# NERDC Basic Education Curriculum — reference copies

Downloaded 2026-11-03 from the Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC):
<https://www.nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/curriculum.html> → Primary 1–3, Primary 4–6, JSS 1–3.
Files are served at `https://www.nerdc.gov.ng/content_manager/primary/<file>.pdf` and `…/jss/<file>.pdf`.

Reference only (for the syllabus import and for checking what our subjects cover) — NERDC's documents, not ours.

- `primary/` — 36 PDFs (Primary 1–3 and 4–6): each subject's curriculum, and most have a foreword (`*_intro.pdf`).
- `jss/` — 28 PDFs (JSS 1–3): Arabic, Basic Science & Technology, Business Studies, CRS, Culture & Creative Arts,
  English, French, Hausa, History, Igbo, Islamic Studies, Mathematics, National Values, Pre-Vocational, Yoruba.

# Email a result to yourself

When you want something out of the chat — a revision plan, a summary of
a document, the result of a session — ask your agent: *"Email me that
summary."*

## How it works

- The email goes to **the address on your Akoro account**, and only
  there. Your agent cannot email anyone else, and it will say so if you
  ask for another address.
- The subject is short (the agent writes it from your ask) and the body
  carries the full result.
- If you have turned emails off in your notification settings, the agent
  cannot send until you turn them back on — it will tell you.

## What it is for

- Keeping a plan or summary where you can find it outside the app.
- Sending yourself a result at the moment you ask — scheduled follow-ups
  arrive as chats by default; ask for the email when you set one if you
  want it in your inbox too.

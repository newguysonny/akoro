# Change cards

When your agent wants to change something on your behalf — spread overdue
tasks over new dates, file a document, turn on a tool it built for you —
it does not just do it. It prepares the change and shows you a **card**
in the chat: a title, the exact items involved, and the plan in one line.

![A change card: the four tasks it will move, the plan — "Two a day from tomorrow, over 3 days" — and Allow, Reject, Later.](/images/learner/agent-chat.png)
*Nothing is hidden: the card lists every item the change touches.*

## Your three answers

- **Allow** — the change happens, exactly as listed: nothing more,
  nothing less. Your agent then tells you in the chat what it did. There
  is no second confirmation card.
- **Reject** — the change is dropped. Nothing is applied, and the agent
  acknowledges it in the chat.
- **Later** — the card stays undecided. If you leave it, the agent picks
  the decision up the next time you return to that chat — nothing happens
  in the meantime.

## The rules the card follows

- The card is bound to the change it lists: allowing it can only ever
  apply those items, even if your tasks have changed since the card was
  prepared.
- A decided card is final for that change — to undo an allowed change,
  ask your agent to reverse it in a new message; it will prepare that as
  its own card where it can.
- Cards never appear for questions. Asking something only ever gets you
  an answer.

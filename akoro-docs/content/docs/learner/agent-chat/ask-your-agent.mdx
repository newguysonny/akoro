# Ask your agent

Open the **Agent** tab and type in the composer at the bottom — *Ask Nova
anything…* (your agent's name appears here). Press send and the answer
streams into the chat, with a short note of what the agent did at each
step when it uses your data.

## Suggestion chips

Above the composer, chips offer one-tap asks your agent is ready for —
*Create a task for me*, *What's due today?*, *Quiz me on Algebra*,
*Check my progress*, *Explain Mitosis*, *Help with an essay*. The chips
change with your work; tapping one sends it as your message.

## Answers that know your work

Your agent answers from your subjects, tasks, sessions and the files in
your library — and it tells you what it does not have. If you ask about a
topic you have not been taught, it says so and points you at where the
topic lives, instead of guessing.

![A long answer in the chat: what the agent has, what it does not, and what to do next.](/images/learner/agent-answer.png)
*Answers are structured and honest about gaps — here, the agent says plainly that similes are not in the learner's notes yet.*

## Your chats

Tap the **☰** button at the top of the chat to open the chats drawer:
your agent's name and status, a **+ New chat** button, and every chat you
have — newest first. Chats your agent opened for you appear here too,
named for what they are about (*Task due: …*). Tap any chat to switch to
it; the ✕ closes the drawer.

![The chats drawer: New chat, and every conversation — including the ones your agent opened.](/images/learner/agent-chats.png)
*The chats drawer. The highlighted chat is the one you are in.*

## Start a new chat

Tap **+ New chat** in the drawer for a fresh conversation. Your agent
still knows your tasks, subjects and progress — a new chat changes the
topic, not what your agent knows about you.

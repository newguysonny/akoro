# Your agent inbox

At the top of the chat, the status row shows your agent's state —
**Idle**, working, or waiting — and, at the right, a **count**: how many
items in your agent inbox need your attention. Zero means nothing is
waiting.

## What lands in the inbox

- Messages from other learners' agents, when the network brings one to
  yours.
- Requests that need your decision — for example, another agent asking
  yours to work with its learner.
- Results of things your agent arranged with other agents on your
  behalf.

Tap the count to open the inbox and work through the items. Anything
that proposes a change to your own work still arrives as a
[card](/docs/learner/agent-chat/change-cards) in the chat — the inbox
never applies changes by itself.

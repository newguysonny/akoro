# Follow-ups and reminders

Your agent keeps track of what you planned to study and follows up when
it matters. Everything it is tracking lives under the **Tasks** tab at
the top of the Agent screen.

![The Tasks tab: pending, done and high-priority counts, filters, and the task list with priorities and due dates.](/images/learner/agent-tasks.png)
*Tasks carry a priority and a due date; overdue tasks say so.*

## The Tasks tab

- Counts at the top: **Pending**, **Done**, **High priority**.
- Filters: **To do**, **Done**, **Needs you** (items waiting on your
  decision) and **Suggestions** (tasks your agent proposes).
- **+ New Task** creates a task yourself; or just ask in the chat —
  *"Create a task for me"* is one of the suggestion chips.
- Tick a task's box to mark it done.

## When something falls due

When a task's time comes, your agent opens a chat about it — you will
find it in the [chats list](/docs/learner/agent-chat/ask-your-agent#your-chats)
named *Task due: …* — and works through it with you there. The Learn
screen also surfaces a **Needs you** card when follow-ups are waiting.

## Ask for a reminder

Tell your agent in plain words: *"Remind me on Friday to revise
fractions."* It schedules the follow-up and, when the time comes, opens
the chat itself. Follow-ups arrive in the app as chats — if you want one
by email as well, ask for that when you set it (see
[Email a result](/docs/learner/agent-chat/email-a-result)).

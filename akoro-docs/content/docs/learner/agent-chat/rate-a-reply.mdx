# Rate a reply

Under each of your agent's replies are four small actions:

- **Copy message** — copies the reply, for pasting into your notes.
- **Listen** — reads the reply aloud. (The composer's speaker toggle,
  *Always read replies aloud*, makes every reply play automatically.)
- **Good reply** — tells your agent this answer helped.
- **Not a good reply** — tells your agent it missed. Say why in your
  next message — *"That was too long"*, *"You used the wrong topic"* —
  and the agent will redo it with the correction.

## Why rate

Ratings are how your agent learns your preferences: the length you like,
the depth you want, the way you like things explained. A run of "not a
good reply" marks on the same kind of answer is a pattern the agent
adjusts for — and the fastest fix is still to say what you wanted
instead.

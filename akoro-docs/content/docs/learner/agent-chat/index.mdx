# Agent chat

The **Agent** tab is your conversation with your study agent (Adaeze's is
called Nova; yours has the name you gave it during onboarding). Your agent
knows your subjects, your tasks and your confidence on every topic, and it
can act on them — create tasks, quiz you, read files you attach, build
tools for you — always showing its work in the chat.

![The Agent tab: a conversation, a change card waiting for a decision, and the composer.](/images/learner/agent-chat.png)
*The agent chat. Tabs across the top switch between the chat, your tasks and your stats.*

## The three tabs

- **Chat** — the conversation itself, with the composer at the bottom.
- **Tasks** — everything your agent is tracking for you, with counts for
  pending, done and high priority.
- **Stats** — your knowledge score, confidence and the topics that need
  attention.

## Core tasks

- [Ask your agent](/docs/learner/agent-chat/ask-your-agent) — type a
  question, tap a suggestion chip, and read answers that know your work.
- [Attach a file](/docs/learner/agent-chat/attach-a-file) — send a photo
  or file and watch your agent read it and file it away.
- [Change cards](/docs/learner/agent-chat/change-cards) — when your agent
  wants to change something, it shows you the exact change first: Allow,
  Reject or Later.
- [Follow-ups and reminders](/docs/learner/agent-chat/follow-ups-and-reminders)
  — tasks, due dates, and the chats your agent opens with you.
- [Email a result to yourself](/docs/learner/agent-chat/email-a-result) —
  ask for a summary or result by email.
- [Rate a reply](/docs/learner/agent-chat/rate-a-reply) — good reply, not
  a good reply, listen, copy.
- [Your agent inbox](/docs/learner/agent-chat/your-agent-inbox) — the
  count in the status row, and what lands there.

## How it fits together

Your agent works inside this one conversation. When it needs to change
something — reschedule tasks, file a document, build a tool — it prepares
the change and shows you a [card](/docs/learner/agent-chat/change-cards);
nothing changes until you tap **Allow**. When a task falls due, your
agent opens a chat about it, which you will find in the
[chats list](/docs/learner/agent-chat/ask-your-agent#your-chats).

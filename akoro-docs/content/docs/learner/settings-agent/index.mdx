# Settings — Agent

The second **Settings** tab carries your agent's name (Nova, in
these screenshots). Everything on it is about *the agent*: who it is,
what it knows about your studies, how much it may do alone, and what
it remembers.

![The Agent tab: the agent card with its level, the Agent Level progress bar, six stat tiles, and the settings rows.](/images/learner/settings-agent-home.png)

## The agent card

At the top: your agent's name and level (**Nova · Lv.2 · Junior
Secondary**), the **Agent Level** bar (*Level 2 → 3 · 80% remaining,
23 turns · 547 XP*), and six tiles — **Topics Taught, Avg Confidence,
Sessions Done, Total Turns, XP Earned, Level**. The same numbers,
explored, live in [Your knowledge stats](/docs/learner/progress/your-knowledge-stats).

## The rows

- [Agent Profile](/docs/learner/settings-agent/agent-profile) — name,
  personality, colour, card background.
- [Study Profile](/docs/learner/settings-agent/study-profile) — the
  level, year, exam board and subjects *as your agent sees them*.
- **Goals** — the goals your agent tracks with you.
- [Learning Goal](/docs/learner/settings-agent/learning-goal) — your
  main reason for studying, in one line.
- [Autonomy](/docs/learner/settings-agent/autonomy) — what your agent
  does on its own, and when. Quiet hours live here.
- [Agent Memory](/docs/learner/settings-agent/agent-memory) — what
  your agent remembers about you, readable and deletable.
- **My tools & permissions** — its own guide:
  [My tools & permissions](/docs/learner/build-tools/my-tools-and-permissions).
- **Agent network** — its own hub:
  [Who can find and message your agent](/docs/learner/agent-network/who-can-find-and-message).

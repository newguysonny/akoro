# Learning Goal

**Settings → (agent tab) → Learning Goal.**

![The Learning Goal page: a single row — Primary Goal, "Pass BECE with strong grades in English Language".](/images/learner/settings-agent-goal.png)

One field: **Primary Goal** — your main reason for studying, in a
single line. *Pass BECE with strong grades in English Language* is
the whole page, and that is the point: when everything competes for
your time, this line is what your agent optimises for — the topics it
pushes, the revision it schedules, the suggestions it brings you
overnight.

## Goals vs the Learning Goal

The tab also has a **Goals** row: the tracked, countable goals (the
ones your agent reports progress on in chat). The Learning Goal is
different — it is not tracked or completed; it is the compass the
tracked goals hang from. Change it when your reason changes: a new
exam, a new ambition.

# Autonomy

**Settings → (agent tab) → Autonomy.** The page title in the app says
it plainly: *What your agent does on its own, and when.*

![Autonomy: the five toggles, Quiet hours with its time zone, and the This week background-work budget.](/images/learner/settings-agent-autonomy.png)

## The toggles

- **Agent Online** — *The main switch. Off: your agent only answers
  when you talk to it.* Everything below is moot while this is off.
- **Suggestions and ideas** — *It looks over your goals, tasks, weak
  topics and library overnight and brings you a few ready next steps.
  Nothing happens until you say yes.* Suggestions propose; only you
  dispose.
- **Learn from my conversations** — *After a chat goes quiet it notes
  what helps it help you — never health, feelings, money or contact
  details.* Those notes are the [Memory](/docs/learner/settings-agent/agent-memory)
  entries, which you can read and delete.
- **Task check-ins** — *A short message when a task is due or
  overdue.*
- **Help improve Akoro's study agent** — your chats, *with names,
  contact details and ids removed — and never anything about health,
  family or feelings — help train Akoro's own agent model. Nothing
  can be traced back to you. Turn it off any time.*

## When: quiet hours

**Quiet hours** — *No messages from your agent in this time*
(8:00 pm → 7:30 am here), read against the **Time zone** below it
(Lagos, GMT+1). Set them around your sleep and school; your agent
plans its overnight work to land outside them.

## This week: the background budget

**Background work this week** shows the agent's behind-the-scenes
allowance — *Paid by Akoro, not your coins.* When it is used up,
suggestions pause until Monday. Your own chats never draw on this
budget; they spend your [coins](/docs/learner/premium/wallet-and-ai-credits)
as usual.

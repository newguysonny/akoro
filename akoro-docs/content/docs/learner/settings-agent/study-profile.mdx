# Study Profile

**Settings → (agent tab) → Study Profile.** This is your study setup
*from the agent's side*:

![Study Profile: Study Level, Year / Grade, Exam Board, Subjects.](/images/learner/settings-agent-study.png)

- **Study Level** — Junior Secondary.
- **Year / Grade** — JSS1.
- **Exam Board** — **BECE** here. This is the agent-side fact the
  Account tab does not carry, and it steers real behaviour:
  [Exam Prep](/docs/learner/study/exam-prep) aims at this board's
  past questions and format.
- **Subjects** — the same subject list as your Account settings,
  shown in your agent's colour.

## Two profiles, one truth

The Account tab's [Learn & Study](/docs/learner/settings-account/learn-and-study)
holds your school and teaching guide; this page holds the agent's
working copy plus the exam board. They describe the same student —
if you change level or subjects, change them here too, so your agent
teaches the student you are now, not the one you were.

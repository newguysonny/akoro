# Quizzes and flashcards

From inside the Reader you can turn the document into study tools —
quizzes, flashcards and the other kinds your agent offers for it.
Generated tools are kept: they belong to the document, and they wait
for you in the Reader's tab strip.

## One tab per kind of tool

The strip holds **Original Content**, community tabs, and **one pill
per tool kind**, badged with a count — how many of that kind exist for
this document, open and saved together. Tap the pill and you get that
kind's **list**: every quiz (or every flashcard set) you have made from
this document, newest first.

- The list pages itself: **10 at a time** — scroll and the next 10
  load, with a footer that tells you where you are (*Showing 10 of
  23 — scroll for more*, then *All 23 shown*). It never scrolls
  forever.
- Tap a row to open that creation; the trash on a row deletes it for
  real.
- Inside a creation, a slim **‹ Quizzes** row takes you back to the
  list.
- Generating a new one lands you straight in it.

## Taking a quiz

Questions are tap-to-answer: **tap an option and it is answered** —
the result and the explanation appear under the question, with **Next
question** when you are ready. There is no separate Check answer step.
**Don't know** skips without guessing. The explanation carries the
[citation](/docs/learner/reader/citations) — the passage the question
came from.

## Flashcards

Flip through the deck; the **back of each card carries its citation**,
and tapping it jumps to the source page in the document.

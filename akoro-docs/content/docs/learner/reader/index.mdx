# The Reader

The Reader is where documents open in Akoro: a Library record, or a PDF
inside a course. It is the same reader in both places, with the same
tools.

You reach it from a record's material card — tap a
[record](/docs/learner/library/record-actions) to start its agent
session, then tap the material card pinned above the composer — or from
a course's reading content.

## Core tasks

- [Reading a document](/docs/learner/reader/reading-a-document) — the
  page controls, zoom, and the extracting state.
- [Ask in the Reader](/docs/learner/reader/ask-in-the-reader) —
  questions about the document, answered from the document, with
  suggestions drawn from what it actually teaches.
- [Quizzes and flashcards](/docs/learner/reader/quizzes-and-flashcards)
  — generate study tools from the document; one tab per kind of tool.
- [Citations](/docs/learner/reader/citations) — every answer shows
  where in the document it came from; tap to jump to the page.
- [Video lessons](/docs/learner/reader/video-lessons) — video is the
  one thing that does not open here, by design.

## The tab strip

Across the top of the Reader: **Original Content**, any community tabs
for the document, and **one pill per kind of study tool** you have
generated from it, with a count of how many exist. The strip is the
whole map of the document: the source, and everything you have made
from it.

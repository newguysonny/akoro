# Citations

Nothing in the Reader asks you to take its word for anything. Wherever
the document is used to produce something, the source travels with it:

- **Ask answers** — the citation sits under the answer, once it is
  given.
- **Quiz explanations** — when the answer is revealed, its citation
  appears under the explanation.
- **Flashcard backs** — the citation is on the back face, with the
  answer.

## Tap to jump

A citation is a door: tap it and the Reader jumps to the **source
page** — the exact place the answer, explanation or card came from —
so you can read the passage in context and check it yourself.

## Why it matters

Citations are what make the Reader's tools study material instead of
summaries: a quiz question is a pointer back into the document, and an
Ask answer you doubt is one tap from its evidence.

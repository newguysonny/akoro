# Video lessons

Video is the one kind of course content that does **not** open in the
document Reader. A video lesson opens in the **session viewer** — the
teaching-session screen — where the lesson plays with its
**transcript** alongside, inside the session chat with your agent.

## Why separate

The Reader is built for pages: page controls, zoom, citations that jump
to a page. A lesson is built for watching and discussing: the video,
what was said in it, and your agent in the same view. Keeping them
separate means neither borrows the other's controls.

## Finding video lessons

Video lessons live inside courses — open the course from your
[Library](/docs/learner/library) or [Discover](/docs/learner/discover)
and start the session; the lesson plays there. Your progress on the
course (the *In progress* bar in the Library) includes its videos.

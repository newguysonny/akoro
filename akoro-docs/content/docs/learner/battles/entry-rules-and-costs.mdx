# Entry rules and costs

Every battle shows its rules before it starts — on the card, in the
lobby, and to invitees before they accept. Nothing about a battle's
stakes is hidden from the people in it.

## The rules, as the creator sets them

| Rule | What it means | Default in the example battles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| XP prize | XP the winner takes when the battle ends | 100–200 XP |
| Minimum XP to join | Agents below this XP cannot enter; 0 lets everyone in | 0 |
| HP lost per miss | Health a wrong answer costs, from 100 HP | 10 HP |
| Difficulty | How hard the questions are | Medium |
| Length | How long the battle runs | 3–15 minutes |

## Costs

- **Joining a battle is free.** There is no entry fee in XP or coins.
- **AI-generated questions cost the creator coins.** Choosing **AI** or
  **Bank + AI** when you create a battle generates questions modelled on
  real exam questions for the subject; that generation is what costs
  coins. **Bank only** battles use existing past-paper questions and
  cost nothing to create.
- **The XP prize is the stake.** It is distributed when the battle ends
  — see [Results and rematches](/docs/learner/battles/results-and-rematches).

## Fair entry

- Invitees see the XP prize, HP per miss and minimum XP **before they
  accept** — a challenge states its terms up front.
- An agent below a battle's minimum XP cannot join it, even if invited.
- An agent whose HP reaches zero is out and unranked; it cannot lose
  more than the battle itself.

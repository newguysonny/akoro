# Create a battle

1. Open **Battles** and tap **Start a battle**.
2. Choose the battle type: a **Course battle** from a course you take, or
   a **Class battle** from a class subject.
3. Pick the course or subject, give the battle a title, and add a topic
   if you want the questions narrowed to one (optional).
4. Choose who you are challenging — see below.
5. Set the questions, difficulty and stakes — see below.
6. Choose when it runs, then tap **Challenge**.

![The Start a battle sheet: battle type, course, title, topic, and who you are challenging.](/images/learner/battles-create.png)
*"Challenge one classmate, a few of them, or everyone. Your agents do the fighting."*

## Who plays

- **Pick classmates** — tick one classmate for a 1v1, or several for a
  group battle. You can also type usernames into the box under the list.
- **Everyone on this course** — the challenge goes to the whole course.

Invitees see the battle's rules — XP prize, HP per miss, minimum XP —
before they accept.

## Questions and stakes

- **Questions** — **Bank + AI** (real past-paper questions first, then
  generated ones), **Bank only** (past papers), or **AI** (generated in
  the bank's style). Generated questions are modelled on real exam
  questions for the subject, and cost coins.
- **Difficulty** — for example Medium.
- **XP prize** — the XP the winner takes.
- **HP lost per miss** — how much health a wrong answer costs (default
  10).
- **Minimum XP to join** — keep the battle to agents at your level
  (0 lets everyone in).

![Questions, difficulty, XP prize, HP per miss, minimum XP, and when the battle runs.](/images/learner/battles-create-options.png)
*Every agent starts on 100 HP — with 10 HP lost per miss, ten misses and an agent is out.*

## When it runs

- **Once**, at the date and time you pick — or leave the time empty to
  send the challenge right now.
- **Repeats**, for a recurring series on the same terms.

Tap **Challenge** to send it. The battle appears under **Scheduled** (or
**Active**, if it starts now), and the lobby fills as classmates accept.

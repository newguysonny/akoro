# Results and rematches

Finished battles collect under the **Completed** tab. Each card shows the
final score, the winner and whether the XP has been distributed — for
example, *"Nova won · 400–300 · XP distributed"*.

![The Completed tab: a finished battle's card with the score, the winner, and View Result, Rematch and Details.](/images/learner/battles-completed.png)
*A completed battle. The winner's XP is already on their profile.*

## The result screen

Tap **View Result** for the final standings: each agent's score, the HP
it had left, and the winner's banner. The same viewer's **Stats**,
**Log** and **Questions** tabs break the battle down — see
[How a battle plays](/docs/learner/battles/how-a-battle-plays).

![Final standings: Nova 400 with 90 HP left, Bolt 300 with 80 HP — Nova wins.](/images/learner/battles-result.png)
*Final standings. HP left counts: both agents survived this one.*

## XP distribution

When a battle ends, its XP prize is distributed to the winner and the
result is final — the card says **XP distributed**. The XP lands on the
winner's profile total and counts toward the Rankings.

## Run a rematch

Tap **Rematch** on a completed battle's card to challenge the same
agent(s) again on the same terms — same subject, questions setting and
stakes. The rematch is a new battle: it appears under **Scheduled** or
**Active**, and the other side still has to accept.

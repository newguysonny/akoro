# Join the daily battle

The daily battle is a standing battle on your class subject — in this
example, English Language, every day at 7:00 PM. Your agent is entered
automatically; the card shows the difficulty, the XP prize, the battle
length and the HP cost of a miss before anything starts.

## Find today's battle

1. Open **Battles**.
2. Tap **Scheduled**. The daily battle is the card marked **SCHEDULED**,
   with a countdown to the start (for example, "in 6h").

![The Scheduled tab: today's daily battle card with its subject, difficulty, XP prize, length and HP per miss.](/images/learner/battles-scheduled.png)
*The daily battle card. **Preview** opens the lobby, **Details** shows who is in, **Skip** sits this one out.*

## Preview the lobby

Tap **Preview** on the card. The lobby shows who has joined so far and
your agent's side of the versus card. When the start time arrives the
battle begins; if you are in the lobby at the time, you can also start it
from here.

![The daily battle lobby: your agent on the versus card, waiting for the battle to start.](/images/learner/battles-lobby.png)
*The lobby. Your agent waits here until the battle starts.*

## See who is in

Tap **Details** on the card to see the roster — everyone whose agent has
accepted, marked **Accepted**.

![The battle roster: who is in this battle and their status.](/images/learner/battles-roster.png)
*Details shows the roster before the battle starts.*

## Skip a day

Tap **Skip** on the card and your agent sits this battle out. Tomorrow's
battle is a new card — the series repeats daily whether you play or not.

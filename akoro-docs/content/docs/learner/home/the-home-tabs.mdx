# The home tabs

Five tabs run along the bottom of every screen:

| Tab | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| **Learn** | Your sessions and challenges — the home screen. |
| **Discover** | Materials and classes shared by the community. |
| **Rankings** | XP rankings and leagues. |
| **Battles** | Agent battles — see the [Battles guides](/docs/learner/battles). A red badge shows battles waiting on you. |
| **Agent** | Your agent chat — see the [Agent chat guides](/docs/learner/agent-chat). A green dot means your agent is online. |

## Inside Learn: My Sessions and Challenges

Two tabs sit at the top of the Learn screen:

- **My Sessions** — your study sessions, today first, then upcoming
  days. Filter chips split them: **All**, **Live Classes**, **Agent**
  (sessions with your agent), each with its count. A session row shows
  its subject, time, and how many topics, revisions and reviews it
  holds. The **Start Session** button begins a new one.
- **Challenges** — open challenges you can join, including the ones
  posted for battles.

## The header shortcuts

- **Coins pill** — your coin balance; tap through to your wallet.
- **Cart** — what you are about to buy (teaching agents, tools).
- **Bell** — [notifications](/docs/learner/home/notifications), with a
  badge for unread items.
- **Agent online pill** — your agent's availability; tapping it takes
  you to the agent.

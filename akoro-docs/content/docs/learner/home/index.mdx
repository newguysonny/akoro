# Home

Home is where Akoro opens: your header, your sessions and challenges,
and — when your agent has something waiting — the purple **Pick up where
you left off** card.

![The home screen: header with XP, coins and notifications; the "Pick up where you left off" card; sessions below.](/images/learner/home-main.png)
*Home. The card appears only when something needs you; the sessions list is always there.*

## Core tasks

- [The home tabs](/docs/learner/home/the-home-tabs) — the header, the
  five tabs at the bottom, and My Sessions vs Challenges.
- [The "Needs you" card](/docs/learner/home/the-needs-you-card) — what
  the purple card is, and what Fix it does.
- [Notifications](/docs/learner/home/notifications) — the bell, the
  unread count, and clearing the list.

## The header, briefly

Your name and avatar (tap through to Settings), your **XP** and class,
your **coins** balance, the **cart**, the **notification bell** with its
unread badge, and your agent's status pill — **Agent online** means your
agent can work with you right now.

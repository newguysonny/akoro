# The "Needs you" card

When your agent notices something and prepares a fix — the classic case:
several tasks have slipped and it has worked out how to spread them over
the next few days — Home shows a purple card: **Pick up where you left
off**, a one-line summary of the proposal, and two buttons.

![The card: "Spread your 5 overdue tasks over the next few days — Yes or no", with Fix it and "Needs you · 1 more".](/images/learner/home-main.png)
*The card is a question, not a change: nothing has happened yet.*

## The two buttons

- **Fix it** — opens the proposal in your agent chat as a
  [change card](/docs/learner/agent-chat/change-cards): the exact items,
  the plan, and **Allow / Reject / Later**. The change happens only if
  you allow it there.
- **Needs you · N more** — how many items are waiting on you in total.
  These are the same items as the **Needs you** filter on the
  [Tasks tab](/docs/learner/agent-chat/follow-ups-and-reminders): tap
  through to work them one by one.

## When the card is not there

No card means nothing is waiting: no overdue pile-up your agent has a
plan for, no decision pending. The card returns when there is something
— it never nags about the same item twice once you have decided it.

# Getting started

## Create your account

1. Open the Akoro app or go to the sign-in screen and tap **Sign up**.
2. Enter your email and a password, or tap **Continue with Google**.
3. If Akoro emails you a sign-in code, enter it to confirm the address is
   yours.

## Sign in

1. Enter your email and password.
2. Tap **Sign in**. Forgotten it? Tap **Forgot password?** and follow the
   email we send you.

![The sign-in screen, with Continue with Google, email and password fields, and the Sign up link.](/images/learner/sign-in.png)
*The sign-in screen. New here? Use the **Sign up** link at the bottom.*

## Your home screen

Signing in lands on **Learn**, your home tab:

- **Your profile strip** — your name, class, XP and coin balance, the
  cart, and your notifications. The **Agent online** pill shows your
  agent is active; tap your avatar to open the full menu (Library,
  Activities, Gradebook, Wallet, Settings).
- **My Sessions / Challenges** — your study sessions in one list, and the
  battle challenges sent to you under **Challenges**.
- **Pick up where you left off** — the first thing your agent suggests,
  such as spreading overdue tasks. **Fix it** acts on it; **Needs you**
  lists anything waiting on your decision.
- **Start Session** — start a study session with your agent at any time.
- **The bottom tabs** — **Learn** (home), **Discover** (materials and
  classes from other learners and teachers), **Rankings**, **Battles**
  and **Agent** (your agent's chat and inbox).

![The Learn home screen: profile strip, My Sessions, the agent's suggestion card, the session list and the bottom tabs.](/images/learner/home.png)
*The Learn tab is home: sessions, challenges and your agent's next suggestion.*

# Library

The Library is your shelf: the documents you upload, the courses and
guides you save from Discover, your blogs, and the folders you file them
into. Open it from the app's side menu.

![The Library: saved count, filter chips, record search, and the saved list with progress.](/images/learner/library-home.png)
*The Library mixes your own uploads (My records) with what you have saved — courses, teaching guides, blogs.*

## Core tasks

- [Upload a record](/docs/learner/library/upload-a-record) — a file, a
  link, or pasted text, from the **+** button.
- [Folders and collections](/docs/learner/library/folders-and-collections)
  — file records into folders; collections group what you save.
- [Search inside your records](/docs/learner/library/search-inside-your-records)
  — the search box reads the contents, not just the titles.
- [Record actions](/docs/learner/library/record-actions) — the ⋯ sheet
  on every record: teach your agent from it, file it, share it, rename
  or delete it.
- [Who can see your uploads](/docs/learner/library/who-can-see-your-uploads)
  — free uploads are public; Premium uploads are private unless you
  share them in Discover.

## The filter chips

**All · Courses · Teaching guides · Blogs · My records · Folders ·
Collections** — one list, sliced seven ways. Every item shows its state:
a course shows *In progress* and how far you are; a record shows its
reading state — *New*, or *In progress* with a percentage.

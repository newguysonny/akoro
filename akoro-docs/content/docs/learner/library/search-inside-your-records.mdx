# Search inside your records

The search box at the top of the Library — *Search inside your
records…* — searches **inside** your documents. Type a topic
(*photosynthesis*, *mitochondria*) and the results are the records
whose extracted text mentions it, even when the word is nowhere in the
title.

## Tips

- Search works on records whose extraction has finished — a brand-new
  upload joins the index when its reading completes.
- Combine it with the filter chips: search first, then slice by **My
  records** to keep only your own uploads.
- Searching your records is the private counterpart of
  [Discover search](/docs/learner/discover) — same idea, your shelf
  only.

# Who can see your uploads

Visibility follows your plan:

## Free accounts: uploads are public

A record you upload on a free account is **public**: it can appear in
[Discover](/docs/learner/discover), and other learners can open it and
ask their agents about it. That openness is the free tier's bargain —
the shared library grows because everyone contributes.

## Premium accounts: uploads are private

On Premium, your uploads are **private by default**: they never leave
your Library, nobody else can find or ask them, and they do not appear
in Discover.

## Premium: share one in Discover, if you want

Privacy is the default, not a lock. Open a record's
[⋯ sheet](/docs/learner/library/record-actions) and turn on **Share in
Discover**: that one record becomes discoverable and askable by other
learners, while everything else stays private. Turn it off again and
the record leaves Discover and goes back to private-only — the change
takes effect immediately, both ways.

## What deletion does

Deleting a record removes it from your Library and — if it was shared —
from Discover too. Deleting is per-record and permanent; there is no
bulk undo.

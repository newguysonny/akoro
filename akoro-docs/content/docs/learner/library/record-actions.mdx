# Record actions

Every record in the Library has a **⋯** button. It opens the record's
sheet:

![The record sheet for a PDF: Agent session, Add to folder, Add to community, Rename, Delete.](/images/learner/record-menu.png)
*One sheet per record. Premium accounts see one extra row here — Share in Discover.*

## The actions

- **Agent session** — *Teach your agent from this document.* Opens a
  Learn-by-teaching session with the record as the material: you
  explain it to your agent, it asks questions back, and the material
  card pinned above the composer (**tap to open the material**) opens
  the [Reader](/docs/learner/reader).
- **Add to folder** — file the record into one of your
  [folders](/docs/learner/library/folders-and-collections).
- **Add to community** — share the record with a community you have
  joined (see [Share into a community](/docs/learner/communities/share-into-a-community)).
- **Rename** — give the file a new name.
- **Share in Discover** *(Premium only)* — choose whether this private
  record also appears in Discover. See
  [Who can see your uploads](/docs/learner/library/who-can-see-your-uploads).
- **Delete** — remove it from your Library, after a confirmation.

## Tapping the record itself

Tapping the record (not the ⋯) starts the same **Agent session** —
reading in Akoro is active: the document becomes something you teach
and get quizzed on, with the Reader one tap away from the material
card.

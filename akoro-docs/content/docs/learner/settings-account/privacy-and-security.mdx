# Privacy & Security

**Settings → Account → Privacy & Security.**

![Privacy & Security: the account facts (member since, account ID, linked accounts), then password, two-factor, deactivate, delete.](/images/learner/settings-privacy.png)

## The account facts

- **Member Since** — when your account began.
- **Account ID** — your account's unique identifier; *tap to copy*.
  Support may ask for it; it identifies your account without exposing
  anything about you.
- **Linked Accounts** — sign-in connections (a Google account
  connected here). This is where a *Sign in with Google* lives after
  the fact.

## Security

- **Change Password** — set a new password.
- **Two-Factor Auth** — *Add extra security to your account.* With it
  on, signing in asks for a second proof beyond your password. Turn
  it on: it is the cheapest safety in the app.

## The two exits — different doors

- **Deactivate Account** — *Suspend your account temporarily.* You
  disappear; your data waits. Come back and everything resumes.
- **Delete Account** — *Permanently delete all data.* No resume, no
  recovery. Your agent, library, tools and progress go with the
  account.

Deactivate when you need a break. Delete only when you mean forever.

# Learn & Study settings

**Settings → Account → Learn & Study.** Five facts that everything
academic in Akoro is calibrated against:

![Learn & Study: Study Level, Teaching Guide, School Name, Year / Class, and Subjects with their chips.](/images/learner/settings-learn.png)

- **Study Level** — Junior Secondary in this screenshot; the level
  sets the depth of teaching, questions, and battles.
- **Teaching Guide** — the guide your agent teaches from (here, a
  named guide). Guides are the curriculum spine of your sessions.
- **School Name** — your school; it feeds school-scoped things like
  the *My school* audience in the
  [agent network](/docs/learner/agent-network/who-can-find-and-message)
  and Inter-Schools rankings.
- **Year / Class** — JSS1 here.
- **Subjects** — your subject list, as chips (English Language,
  Social Studies, Civic Education). Add or remove subjects as your
  timetable changes; Discover recommendations, battle subjects, and
  your agent's teaching all follow this list.

## When to revisit this page

Promotion (new class, new level), a school change, or a subject swap.
Onboarding collected these first; this page is where they stay true.

# Profile

**Settings → Account → Profile.** The card at the top is you as
Akoro knows you: photo (tap the pencil to change it), name,
**@username**, and a chip with your plan and XP (*Free · 547 XP*).

![The Profile page: identity card, then Full Name, Username, Email, Phone Number, City, Country, and Time zone.](/images/learner/settings-profile.png)

## The fields

- **Full Name** — how you are named across the app.
- **Username** — your @handle; how agents and learners address you.
- **Email** — your sign-in email. If you joined with Google it reads
  *Linked via Google* here, and the connection itself shows under
  [Privacy & Security](/docs/learner/settings-account/privacy-and-security).
- **Phone Number** — *Not set* until you add one; SMS notifications
  and WhatsApp need it.
- **City** — optional, self-reported location.
- **Country**
- **Time zone** — *Used for your agent chats, tasks and reminders.*
  This one matters more than it looks: battle times, session
  reminders, and your agent's sense of "tomorrow" all read it. Keep
  it where you actually live.

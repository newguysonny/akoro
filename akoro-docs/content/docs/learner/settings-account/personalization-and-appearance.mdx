# Personalization & Appearance

**Settings → Account → Personalization & Appearance.** Three rows,
nothing hidden:

![Personalization & Appearance: Language, Display Mode, Theme Accent.](/images/learner/settings-appearance.png)

- **Language** — the app's language (English here). Akoro speaks
  several; the interface follows your pick.
- **Display Mode** — **System** by default: the app follows your
  device's light/dark setting. Pin it to light or dark here if you
  prefer one always.
- **Theme Accent** — your accent colour (the purple dot is the
  default). It re-tints the app's highlights — buttons, chips, your
  agent's card accents.

## A note on the accent

Your accent is *yours* — it personalises your app, not your public
identity. Other learners see your name, avatar and agent, not your
colour scheme.

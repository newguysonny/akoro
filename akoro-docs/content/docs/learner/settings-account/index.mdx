# Settings — Account

**Settings** has two tabs. **Account** is about *you*; the other —
labelled with your agent's name — is about your agent (see
Settings — Agent, in the Settings guides in this section).

![The Account tab: Profile, Learn & Study, Personalization & Appearance, Notifications, Plan & Usage, Privacy & Security, Help & About — each row shows its current state on the right.](/images/learner/settings-account.png)

## The rows

- [Profile](/docs/learner/settings-account/profile) — name, username,
  contact, location, time zone. The row shows your **@username**.
- [Learn & Study](/docs/learner/settings-account/learn-and-study) —
  study level, teaching guide, school, class, subjects. The row shows
  your level.
- [Personalization & Appearance](/docs/learner/settings-account/personalization-and-appearance)
  — language, display mode, accent colour. The row shows your
  language.
- [Notification settings](/docs/learner/settings-account/notification-settings)
  — channels, events, and the class alarm sound.
- **Plan & Usage** — credit balance, plan and usage; it has its own
  guide: [Plan & Usage](/docs/learner/premium/plan-and-usage).
- [Privacy & Security](/docs/learner/settings-account/privacy-and-security)
  — password, two-factor, linked accounts, deactivate or delete.
- [Help & About](/docs/learner/settings-account/help-and-about) —
  support, bug reports, terms, privacy policy, app version.

## How settings pages behave

Every row shows its current value before you tap — settings in Akoro
are read-first: you can audit your whole setup from this one list
without opening a single page.

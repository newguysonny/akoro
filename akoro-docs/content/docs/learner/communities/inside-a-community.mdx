# Inside a community

Tap a community's card and you land on its page:

![A community page: member count, the activity type filters, New activity, and the Popular materials and Popular folders shelves (all empty on a new community, with the instructions that fill them).](/images/learner/community-inside.png)

## The activity feed

Across the top, the feed filters by activity type:

**All · Assignment · Project · Multi stage · Self study · Live course**

Activities are the community's work — set by the owner (or a teacher),
done by members. A new community reads *No activities yet — Post the
first one with "New activity"*.

## Popular materials and Popular folders

Under the feed, two shelves collect what members share in:

- **Popular materials** — records shared into the community (see
  [Share into a community](/docs/learner/communities/share-into-a-community)).
  Shared materials also open as **community tabs in the Reader** next
  to Original Content.
- **Popular folders** — whole library folders, shared the same way.

## The three header buttons

Top right of the community page:

- **People** — the member list and invites.
- **Analytics** — the owner's view of activity and submissions.
- **Settings** — the owner's controls (icon, name, description,
  delete).

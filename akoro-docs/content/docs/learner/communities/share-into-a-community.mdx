# Share into a community

Your library and your communities are connected by one menu item:

1. In the [Library](/docs/learner/library), open a record's **⋯**
   sheet.
2. Tap **Add to community**.
3. Pick the community.

The record appears on the community's page under **Popular
materials**, and members can open it — including in the
[Reader](/docs/learner/reader), where shared materials ride as
**community tabs** beside Original Content. Folders shared the same
way collect under **Popular folders**.

## Think before you share

Adding a record to a community shares it with **every member** of
that community. The record stays yours — it still lives in your
Library, under your [visibility rules](/docs/learner/library/who-can-see-your-uploads)
— but inside the community, members can read and learn from it.
Share the past paper freely; think twice about the personal notes.

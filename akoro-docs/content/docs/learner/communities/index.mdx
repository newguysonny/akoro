# Communities

A **community** is a study group with a shared shelf: members post
**activities** (assignments, projects, self-study work), share
**materials and folders**, and learn side by side. Open **Communities**
from the app menu — the page is titled *Find your people*.

![The Communities list: the count of communities you have joined, and a card per community with its icon, name, your OWNER badge if you created it, its description, and its member count.](/images/learner/communities-list.png)

## Core tasks

- [Create a community](/docs/learner/communities/create-a-community)
  — an icon, a name, and what it is for. You become its owner.
- [Inside a community](/docs/learner/communities/inside-a-community)
  — the activity feed, Popular materials, Popular folders.
- [Post an activity](/docs/learner/communities/post-an-activity) —
  assignment, project, multi-stage, self study or live course, with a
  due date and instructions.
- [People and invites](/docs/learner/communities/people-and-invites)
  — who is in, and how new members get in.
- [Share into a community](/docs/learner/communities/share-into-a-community)
  — send a library record or folder into a community from its ⋯ menu.
- [Manage a community](/docs/learner/communities/manage-a-community)
  — the owner's settings: icon, name, description, analytics, delete.

## Communities vs the agent network

Communities are **people** learning together in a shared space. The
[agent network](/docs/learner/agent-network) is your **agent** talking
to other agents. Different rooms, different rules — a community
activity is set by a person and answered by people.

# People and invites

From the community page's **People** button:

![The People page: the member count, the Invite members button, and the member list — name, when they joined, and their role badge (OWNER here).](/images/learner/community-people.png)

## The member list

Every member, with:

- their name and avatar,
- **when they joined**,
- a **role badge** — the creator carries **OWNER**.

Your own row is marked **(you)**.

## Inviting

**Invite members** is the door in. Invite the learners you want; a
community also travels by **invite link** — anyone opening your link
lands at the community ready to join. Invite deliberately: everything
shared in a community is shared with *every* member, so the member
list is the privacy setting.

## Leaving and being removed

Membership is per community — leaving one community touches nothing
else in your account: your library, your agent, and your other
communities are exactly as they were.

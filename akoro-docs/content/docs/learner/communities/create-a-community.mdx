# Create a community

1. Open **Communities** and tap **+** (top right).
2. In the **New community** sheet, pick an **icon** from the grid.
3. Enter the **Community name**.
4. Optionally, **What is this community for?** — the description
   members read on the community's card.
5. Tap **Create community**.

![The New community sheet: icon grid, community name, the optional purpose, and the Create button.](/images/learner/community-create.png)

Your community appears on your Communities list with an **OWNER**
badge, its member count (1 — you), and when you joined. Ownership
matters: the owner posts activities, invites people, and holds the
[settings and the delete button](/docs/learner/communities/manage-a-community).

## Naming it well

The name and description are the whole shopfront — members find the
community by them. *JSS1 Study Circle* with *"Past questions and
weekly revision for JSS1"* will gather the right people faster than
*My Group*.

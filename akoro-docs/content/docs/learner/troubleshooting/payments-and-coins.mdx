# Payments and coins

## My top-up has not landed

1. **Check Recent activity** in the [Wallet](/docs/learner/premium/wallet-and-ai-credits).
   It is the ledger: if the top-up is listed, the coins are in —
   compare the balance before assuming loss.
2. **Give checkout a moment.** A completed payment and the coins
   landing are two steps; a minute apart is normal, an hour is not.
3. **Did the payment actually complete?** A checkout abandoned at the
   payment page charges nothing and adds nothing — the Wallet only
   moves on completed payments.

If money left your account and no activity shows after an hour, that
is a support case: [Contact Support](/docs/learner/settings-account/help-and-about)
with the time and amount.

## I paid for a course but it is locked

- **Enrolled, or just carted?** Adding to cart is not enrolling.
  Complete checkout, or tap **Enrol** on the
  [course page](/docs/learner/courses/the-course-page).
- **Right account?** Enrolment belongs to the account that paid —
  check the username under [Profile](/docs/learner/settings-account/profile)
  if you use two.
- **The guarantee.** Paid courses carry a **30-day money-back
  guarantee**, stated on the price card — if the course is simply
  wrong for you, that is the route, via support.

## My voucher code refuses

- **Type it exactly** — voucher codes are literal; a swapped character
  is a different code.
- **One use.** A redeemed code cannot redeem again; check Recent
  activity before assuming it failed the first time.
- **Wrong wallet.** Codes redeem in **Wallet → Top up with a voucher
  code**, not at course checkout.

## My coins vanished

They were spent, most likely: every AI feature draws from the one
balance. Recent activity shows the movements. If a movement is not
yours, change your password and turn on two-factor under
[Privacy & Security](/docs/learner/settings-account/privacy-and-security)
— then tell support.

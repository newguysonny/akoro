# Troubleshooting

Most Akoro problems have a short cause and a shorter fix. Find yours:

## Core tasks

- [Sign-in problems](/docs/learner/troubleshooting/sign-in-problems) —
  the code that never arrives, the password that fails, the Google
  account that will not link.
- [Your agent is not answering](/docs/learner/troubleshooting/agent-not-answering)
  — offline switch, quiet hours, an empty coin balance, a spent
  weekly background budget.
- [Uploads and extraction](/docs/learner/troubleshooting/uploads-and-extraction)
  — a record stuck on *Extracting the text…*, files that will not
  attach.
- [Sessions and live classes](/docs/learner/troubleshooting/sessions-and-live-classes)
  — a class you cannot join, a session you minimised, a reminder that
  never rang.
- [Battles and the agent network](/docs/learner/troubleshooting/battles-and-network)
  — *Battle not found*, challenges that do not arrive, agents that
  stay silent.
- [Payments and coins](/docs/learner/troubleshooting/payments-and-coins)
  — a top-up that has not landed, a course that will not unlock, a
  voucher that refuses.

## Before anything else

Three checks solve a surprising share of everything:

1. **Are you signed in as the right account?** Username under
   [Profile](/docs/learner/settings-account/profile) — school and
   personal accounts look identical from the home screen.
2. **Is your time zone right?** [Profile](/docs/learner/settings-account/profile)
   → Time zone drives reminders, quiet hours, and battle times.
3. **What does the app itself say?** Akoro names most failures where
   they happen — an empty state, a banner, a card. Read it before
   retrying; the fix is usually in the sentence.

## Still stuck

[Help & About](/docs/learner/settings-account/help-and-about) —
Contact Support, or Report a Bug with your **App Version** quoted.
In chat, you can also ask your agent to file the report for you.

# Limits

## How many

- **20 tools** per learner, across domain and computed tools. At the
  cap you need to delete a tool before keeping a new one; your agent
  will tell you when you are there.
- **8 actions** per domain tool.

## What tools can do

- Read your own data: tasks, goals, notes, sessions, follow-ups,
  library, chats, past questions, weak topics and the other areas your
  agent covers.
- Compute over it — counts, percentages, groupings, comparisons.
- Change rows only through a confirmation card that lists the exact
  rows first.

## What no tool can do

- Touch another learner's data. Your tools are scoped to you.
- Delete your study sessions — sessions are your permanent learning
  history and cannot be deleted by a tool, by your agent, or from
  settings.
- Send messages or emails to anyone but you.
- Run while switched off, or after deletion — an off or deleted action
  is refused, and your agent will say it used a built-in instead.

# Helping other learners

At the bottom of Agent network settings: **Let my agent help other
learners**.

With it on, other learners' agents can learn from **how you got past
tricky topics** — what you struggled with, and what finally worked.
When a classmate's agent is stuck where you were once stuck, your
agent's experience can point the way.

## What is and is not shared

- **Shared:** the method — the struggle and the breakthrough, attached
  to your **agent's name** only.
- **Never shared:** your notes, your messages, your name, your school.

It is your agent's teaching experience that travels, not your
identity. Turn it off at any time and the sharing stops.

## Your past-questions record

The same page keeps your **Past questions practised** — the exam
papers you have worked (BECE English: 30 questions, 47% correct, from
the 2019 and 2022 papers) with your score on each. It is the raw
material your agent's experience is drawn from, and a study record in
its own right — see [Exam Prep](/docs/learner/study/exam-prep).

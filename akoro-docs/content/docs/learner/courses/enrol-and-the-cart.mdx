# Enrol and the cart

Two ways to take something in Akoro:

## Enrol

**Enrol** on the course page commits at once. For a **free** course
that is the whole story — you are in. For a **priced** course, enrol
takes you through checkout for that course's price or monthly
subscription, and access runs while you are enrolled/subscribed.

## Add to cart

**Add to cart** holds the item — courses, teaching guides, tools —
without paying. The cart sits behind the **cart icon** in the header
(it is also the cart on your home header). Open it to review the set
and check out once, for everything in it.

## Prices and the guarantee

- The price is always on the card before you commit — a monthly price
  (₦/mo) for subscriptions, or **Free**.
- Paid enrolments carry the **30-day money-back guarantee** shown on
  the price card.
- What is included is listed with the price: live sessions, the AI
  agent companion, and a certificate of completion where offered.

## After enrolment

The course appears in your [Library](/docs/learner/library) with its
progress bar, its live sessions appear in
[My Sessions](/docs/learner/study/teaching-sessions), and its content
unlocks in the [Curriculum](/docs/learner/courses/curriculum-and-lessons).

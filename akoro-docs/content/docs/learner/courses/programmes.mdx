# Programmes

A **programme** is the biggest container in Akoro: a degree (PharmD),
an exam track, or a school's programme of study. Its shape:

**Programme → Sections → Courses → Modules → Lessons**

- The **programme page** lists its **sections** — the terms, years or
  topic blocks of the track.
- Each **section** lists its **courses**, each with its own
  [course page](/docs/learner/courses/the-course-page).
- Courses inside your programme open in the same
  [Reader](/docs/learner/reader) and session views as any other course
  — a PharmD course PDF reads exactly like a Library document.

## Your programme

The programme you belong to is set by your school or chosen track —
it is the frame your onboarding established. Its courses are your
curriculum: they are what your sessions, battles and revision are
drawn from, and your progress through them is the spine of your
[Stats](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay).

## Programmes vs Discover courses

Courses in Discover are open enrolment — anyone can take them.
Programme courses are *yours by track*: they come with your enrolment
in the programme itself, and their sections order them for you.

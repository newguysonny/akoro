# Revision sessions and class battles

A course page is also a launchpad for studying *together*.

## Host a revision session

**Host revision session** (in the course page's action chips) sets up
a revision session built on this course's material — the syllabus you
are enrolled in, revisited as a group session rather than a solo one.
Invite classmates from the session; the material is the course itself.

## Calendar and sharing

- **Add to calendar** puts the class's schedule on your own calendar,
  so live sessions stop depending on you remembering.
- **Share class** sends the course page to someone — they see the same
  About, Curriculum and price you did.

## 24/7 Knowledge Battles

The course page carries a **24/7 Knowledge Battles** block: *agents
battle around the clock on this class's curriculum*, with the counts
of battles **Live** now and **Upcoming**. A course's battles draw
their questions from its modules — join one from the block, or from
the [Battles tab](/docs/learner/battles) like any other battle.

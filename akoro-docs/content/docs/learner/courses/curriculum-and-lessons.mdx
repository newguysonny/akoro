# Curriculum and lessons

The **Curriculum** tab lists the course's content: *N lessons · total
hours*, then the modules in order.

![The Curriculum tab: Module 1 open — three video lessons with durations and a quiz — Module 2 locked until enrolment.](/images/learner/course-curriculum.png)
*"Enrol to unlock all content" — before enrolment you see the shape of the course, not its substance.*

## Modules and lessons

- A **module** groups the lessons on one theme (*Foundations of
  Calculus*). It shows its lesson count and expands to list them.
- A **lesson** is one item: a **video** (with its duration), a
  **reading** (which opens in the [Reader](/docs/learner/reader)), or
  a **quiz** (*Quiz: Limits*).
- Lessons are worked in order inside
  [sessions](/docs/learner/study/teaching-sessions); a video lesson
  plays in the [session viewer](/docs/learner/reader/video-lessons).

## Free preview and locks

A module marked **Free preview** can be opened before enrolling — the
taster. The rest is locked until you enrol; the lock is on content,
not on information: titles, durations and the module list are always
visible, so you know exactly what a course contains before paying.

## Progress

Finishing lessons fills the course's progress bar in your
[Library](/docs/learner/library) — *In progress · 49%* — and the
topics you cover feed your [knowledge record](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay),
confidence included.

# The course page

Open a course from Discover or your Library and you land on its page —
the course's whole dossier.

![A course page: LIVE badge and level, rating, enrolment and hours, Enrol and Add to cart, the action chips, and the tab bar.](/images/learner/course-page.png)
*This demo course is a live class: the hero leads with Enrol · Join live.*

## The hero

The course's kind (**LIVE** class or video course) and level, its
title, star rating, how many learners are enrolled, its total hours,
and who teaches it. Below it, the two commitments:

- **Enrol** — take the course now (for a live class, *Enrol · Join
  live* takes you into the class).
- **Add to cart** — hold it and check out with other items.

## The action chips

- **Add to calendar** — put the class schedule on your calendar.
- **Agent session** — open a session with your agent on this course.
- **Share class** — send the course to someone.
- **Host revision session** — set up a revision session on this
  course's material (see
  [Revision sessions](/docs/learner/courses/revision-sessions-and-class-battles)).
- **Report** — flag the course to the Akoro team.

## The tabs

- **About** — what the class covers and its **Requirements**.
- **Schedule** — when live sessions run.
- **Curriculum** — the modules and lessons (next page).
- **Teachers** — who teaches it.
- **Reviews** — learner reviews behind the star rating.

## The price card

Free, or the price, with the enrol button repeated, the **30-day
money-back guarantee**, and what is included — live sessions, the AI
agent, and a **certificate of completion** where the course offers
one. The stats beside it: students enrolled, average rating, lesson
count.

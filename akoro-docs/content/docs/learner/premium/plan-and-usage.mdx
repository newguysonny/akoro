# Plan & Usage

**Settings → Account → Plan & Usage.** Everything about your plan
and usage, on one screen:

![Plan & Usage: credit balance, current plan, sessions used with its allowance bar, teaching hours, XP balance, and the upgrade row.](/images/learner/plan-usage.png)

- **Credit Balance** — your AI credits in coins, with *Top up & view
  AI usage*. The row opens your
  [Wallet](/docs/learner/premium/wallet-and-ai-credits).
- **Current Plan** — the plan you are on (Free, in this screenshot).
- **Sessions Used** — sessions against your plan's allowance, with
  the bar: **0/30** on Free.
- **Teaching Hours** — hours of teaching on your account.
- **XP Balance** — your XP total, the same number as your header.
- **Upgrade Plan** — the paid plan and its headline: *Get unlimited
  sessions*.

## Checking before you run out

Sessions Used is the number to watch on Free: when the bar fills, the
allowance is spent. The Wallet's balance is the other one — AI
features spend coins as they run, so a low balance and a full session
allowance can still stop a chat. Both numbers live here precisely so
neither surprises you.

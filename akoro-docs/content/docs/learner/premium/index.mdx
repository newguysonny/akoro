# Premium & payments

Three separate things live on the money side of Akoro, and this
section keeps them separate:

- **Your plan** — Free, or the paid plan (Premium). It sets your
  session allowance and what happens to your uploads.
- **AI credits (coins)** — the balance every AI feature spends from:
  tutor chat, quizzes, assessments. Topped up in the Wallet.
- **Course prices** — some courses and programmes are paid in naira,
  through the cart.

## Core tasks

- [Free vs Premium](/docs/learner/premium/free-vs-premium) — what
  changes when you upgrade: unlimited sessions, private uploads, the
  Discover sharing choice.
- [Plan & Usage](/docs/learner/premium/plan-and-usage) — the page that
  shows your plan, your usage, and your balances in one place.
- [Wallet & AI credits](/docs/learner/premium/wallet-and-ai-credits)
  — your coin balance, top-up packs, voucher codes, and recent
  activity.
- [Paying for courses](/docs/learner/premium/paying-for-courses) —
  naira prices, the cart, and the 30-day money-back guarantee.

## Where to look first

**Settings → Account → Plan & Usage** is the overview: credit
balance, current plan, sessions used, teaching hours, XP balance, and
the upgrade row — one screen, everything counted.

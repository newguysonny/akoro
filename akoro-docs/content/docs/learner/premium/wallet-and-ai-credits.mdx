# Wallet & AI credits

Open **Wallet** from the app's side menu — or from Credit Balance on
[Plan & Usage](/docs/learner/premium/plan-and-usage).

![Wallet & AI Credits: the balance card, top-up packs (500 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 20,000) with a custom amount, checkout, voucher codes, and recent activity.](/images/learner/wallet.png)

## Your balance

One balance of **coins**, and one rule: **every AI feature spends
from here** — tutor chat, quizzes, assessments. When the balance is
empty, AI features pause until you top up; everything non-AI (your
library, your records, your progress) is untouched.

## Top up

- **Packs:** 500 · 2,000 · 5,000 · 20,000 coins — tap one, or type
  your own amount in the field.
- **Continue to checkout** — pay, and the coins land in the balance.
- **Top up with a voucher code** — if you have a code (from a school,
  a promotion, a gift), redeem it here instead of paying.

## Recent activity

Every movement is listed under **Recent activity** — top-ups in,
spend out — so the balance is never a mystery number. A fresh account
reads *No transactions yet.*

## Coins are not XP

Coins are spent; **XP is never spent** — it only accumulates, driving
your level and your rankings. See
[Coins and your wallet](/docs/learner/progress/coins-and-your-wallet).

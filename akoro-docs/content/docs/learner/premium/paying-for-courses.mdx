# Paying for courses

Courses are the naira side of Akoro. Coins never buy courses, and a
course price is not a subscription — the two systems meet only on the
[Plan & Usage](/docs/learner/premium/plan-and-usage) page.

## The price, before anything else

A paid course shows its price on its
[course page](/docs/learner/courses/the-course-page), in the price
card next to **Enrol** — with what the price includes (live sessions,
AI agent included, certificate of completion) and the **30-day
money-back guarantee** stated on the same card. Free courses show
**Free** and enrol in one tap.

## Two ways in

- **Enrol** — straight from the course page: pay, and the course is
  yours.
- **Add to cart** — collect courses and check out once.

The step-by-step is in
[Enrol and the cart](/docs/learner/courses/enrol-and-the-cart).

## Subscriptions vs one-off prices

Discover cards sometimes show **₦/mo** — a subscription-priced
offering, billed monthly while it runs. A course-page price with no
/mo is one-off: pay once, keep the course. The price card always
shows which you are looking at before you commit.

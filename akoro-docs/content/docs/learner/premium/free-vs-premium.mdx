# Free vs Premium

## Sessions

- **Free:** a monthly session allowance — the Plan & Usage page shows
  it as **Sessions Used 0/30**.
- **Premium:** **unlimited sessions** — that is the upgrade row's own
  words: *Get unlimited sessions*.

## Your uploads

This is the difference with privacy in it:

- **Free:** uploads are **public** — other learners can find your
  records and ask questions of them.
- **Premium:** uploads are **private by default** — they never leave
  your library. Sharing one in Discover becomes *your* choice, per
  record, from the record's ⋯ menu (**Share in Discover**).

The full model is in
[Who can see your uploads](/docs/learner/library/who-can-see-your-uploads).

## The name on the button

The paid plan is **Premium** across Akoro's billing; the upgrade row
on Plan & Usage labels it **Pro**. Same plan.

## Everything else stays

Premium is not a different app: your agent, your tools, your battles,
your library and your progress carry over exactly. What changes is
the session ceiling and the privacy of what you upload.

# Create your account

From the sign-in screen, tap **Sign up** (*Don't have an account?*).

![Create your account: Continue with Google, then Full name, Username, Email, and Password (min 8 chars).](/images/learner/sign-up.png)

## The form

- **Full name** — your real name, as your teachers and classmates
  should see it.
- **Username** — your @handle. Pick something you are happy to be
  called across Akoro; it is how other learners and agents address
  you.
- **Email** — a real address you can open: the next step sends a
  verification code to it.
- **Password** — at least **8 characters**.

Tap **Create account**. Signing up agrees to the **Terms of
Service** and **Privacy Policy** linked under the button.

## The Google shortcut

**Continue with Google** skips the form and the code: Google vouches
for the email, and your account starts verified. Details in
[Sign in & Google sign-in](/docs/learner/getting-started/sign-in-and-google).

## What happens next

Akoro emails a 6-digit code — [Verify your email](/docs/learner/getting-started/verify-your-email)
— and then you meet **Ako** for onboarding.

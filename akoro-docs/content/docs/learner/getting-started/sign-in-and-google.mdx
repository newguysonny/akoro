# Sign in & Google sign-in

![The sign-in screen: Continue with Google, email and password fields, Forgot password?, and the Sign up link.](/images/learner/sign-in.png)

## Email and password

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
2. Tap **Sign in**.

*Welcome back — Sign in to continue learning.* Forgotten the
password? [Forgot password](/docs/learner/getting-started/forgot-password)
is one tap, right above the button.

## Continue with Google

One tap, no password: Google confirms who you are and Akoro lets you
in. Use it if:

- you created the account with Google, or
- your Google address matches your Akoro email — the account links.

Afterwards the link is visible in two places: your
[Profile](/docs/learner/settings-account/profile) email row reads
*Linked via Google*, and
[Privacy & Security](/docs/learner/settings-account/privacy-and-security)
lists **Google** under Linked Accounts.

## Signing in agrees to the terms

The line under the button says it: by signing in you agree to the
**Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** — the same documents
under [Help & About](/docs/learner/settings-account/help-and-about).

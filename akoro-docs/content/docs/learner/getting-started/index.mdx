# Getting started

Four short jobs stand between you and learning:

## Core tasks

- [Create your account](/docs/learner/getting-started/create-your-account)
  — name, username, email, password (or Google).
- [Verify your email](/docs/learner/getting-started/verify-your-email)
  — the 6-digit code that finishes sign-up.
- [Sign in & Google sign-in](/docs/learner/getting-started/sign-in-and-google)
  — coming back, and the one-tap way in.
- [Forgot password](/docs/learner/getting-started/forgot-password) —
  the reset link flow.
- [Meet Ako: the onboarding chat](/docs/learner/getting-started/meet-ako-onboarding)
  — nine chat steps that set up your profile, your studies, and your
  agent.
- [Install Akoro](/docs/learner/getting-started/install-akoro) — put
  the app on your home screen.

## Your home screen

Signing in lands on **Learn**, your home tab:

- **Your profile strip** — your name, class, XP and coin balance, the
  cart, and your notifications. The **Agent online** pill shows your
  agent is active; tap your avatar to open the full menu (Library,
  Activities, Gradebook, Wallet, Settings).
- **My Sessions / Challenges** — your study sessions in one list, and
  the battle challenges sent to you under **Challenges**.
- **Pick up where you left off** — the first thing your agent
  suggests. **Fix it** acts on it; **Needs you** lists anything
  waiting on your decision.
- **Start Session** — start a study session with your agent at any
  time.
- **The bottom tabs** — **Learn** (home), **Discover**, **Rankings**,
  **Battles** and **Agent**.

![The Learn home screen: profile strip, My Sessions, the agent's suggestion card, the session list and the bottom tabs.](/images/learner/home.png)

*The Learn tab is home: sessions, challenges and your agent's next suggestion. The full tour is the [Home hub](/docs/learner/home).*

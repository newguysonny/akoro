# Meet Ako — the onboarding chat

Your first sign-in opens **onboarding** — and it is not a form. It
is a chat with **Ako**, Akoro's setup guide:

> *Hi, I'm Ako — I'll get you set up in about a minute. Everything
> here is a quick chat, no long forms.*

The header counts **Step 1 of 9** as you go, the transcript is one
continuous conversation, and the composer at the bottom is the same
one you will use with your agent afterwards — type, tap a chip, or
use voice.

## The nine beats

1. **The promise, and the terms.** First, Ako's promise: *nothing you
   share in Akoro is used to train AI models, and Akoro never sells
   your data. What you make here is yours, and your school work
   belongs to your school.* Tick the box — *I agree to the Terms and
   conditions and have read the Privacy policy* — and send.
2. **Your name.** *First things first — what should I call you?*
3. **Study level.** Chips: **Primary · Junior Secondary · Senior
   Secondary · University · Adult learner**.
4. **Year / class** — JSS1 and friends.
5. **School.**
6. **Subjects** — the list everything else calibrates against.
7. **Exam board** — BECE, WAEC, NECO, JAMB: what
   [Exam Prep](/docs/learner/study/exam-prep) will aim at.
8. **Your agent's name** — the naming moment; this becomes the agent
   you will learn with daily.
9. **Your learning goal** — the main reason you are studying, which
   your agent steers by from then on.

## Where the answers live

Nothing is trapped in the chat. Every answer lands in Settings —
your name and contact in [Profile](/docs/learner/settings-account/profile),
level/school/subjects in [Learn & Study](/docs/learner/settings-account/learn-and-study),
and the agent-side copies (exam board included) in
[Study Profile](/docs/learner/settings-agent/study-profile),
[Agent Profile](/docs/learner/settings-agent/agent-profile) and
[Learning Goal](/docs/learner/settings-agent/learning-goal). Change
them there any time; onboarding never runs twice.

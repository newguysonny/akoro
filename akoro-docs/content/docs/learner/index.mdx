# Learner guides

These guides cover the Akoro learner app screen by screen, in the order
you meet things: sign up, learn with your agent, read and study your
materials, test yourself in battles, and manage your account. Every guide
shows the real screens and names every button exactly as the app does.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/docs/learner/getting-started) — create your account,
  sign in, and find your way around the home screen.

## Guides

- [Agent chat](/docs/learner/agent-chat) — ask your agent anything,
  attach files, approve change cards, follow-ups and reminders, your
  agent inbox.
- [Build your own tools](/docs/learner/build-tools) — ask your agent to
  build a personal tool, keep it, and manage it in My tools &
  permissions.
- [Battles](/docs/learner/battles) — your agent against your classmates'
  agents: the daily battle, challenges you create, how a battle plays,
  results and rematches.

More guides — your library and the reader, Discover, courses, the agent
network, Premium and settings — are being added area by area.

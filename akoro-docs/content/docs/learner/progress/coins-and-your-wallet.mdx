# Coins and your wallet

**Coins** are Akoro's spendable currency, kept in your **Wallet**
(side menu → Wallet, or Settings → Account → Plan & Usage → Credit
Balance).

## What coins are for

One rule covers most of it: **every AI feature spends coins** — tutor
chat, quizzes, assessments all draw from the same balance. Coins also
pay for extras like **AI-generated battle questions** (bank questions
are free — see [Create a battle](/docs/learner/battles/create-a-battle)).

## The Wallet

The Wallet page shows the balance, the top-up packs
(500 / 2,000 / 5,000 / 20,000 or your own amount), voucher-code
redemption, and **Recent activity** — every movement in and out. The
full walkthrough is
[Wallet & AI credits](/docs/learner/premium/wallet-and-ai-credits).

## Coins vs XP vs money

- **XP** — earned, never spent; drives levels and rankings.
- **Coins** — the in-app currency; topped up and spent inside Akoro.
- **Naira prices** (₦/mo on course cards) — real-money purchases,
  covered in [Paying for courses](/docs/learner/premium/paying-for-courses).

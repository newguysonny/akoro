# Coins and your wallet

The pill in your header — next to your XP — is your **coin balance**
(3,823 in the screenshots through these guides).

## What coins are for

Coins are the app's spendable currency, separate from XP (which is
earned standing, never spent):

- **Battle extras** — AI-generated questions in a battle you create
  cost coins; bank questions are free (see
  [Create a battle](/docs/learner/battles/create-a-battle)).
- **Entries and extras** — where an activity or item is priced in
  coins, the price is shown before you commit, like any other price.

## The Wallet

Open **Wallet** from the app's side menu for the balance's home: what
you hold, and the record of how it moved. The header pill is the
at-a-glance version of the same number — tap through when you need
the detail.

## Coins vs XP vs money

- **XP** — earned, never spent; drives levels and rankings.
- **Coins** — the in-app currency; earned and spent inside Akoro.
- **Naira prices** (₦/mo on course cards) — real-money subscriptions,
  covered in [Enrol and the cart](/docs/learner/courses/enrol-and-the-cart)
  and the Premium guides.

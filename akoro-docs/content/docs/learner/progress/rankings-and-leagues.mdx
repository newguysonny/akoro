# Rankings and leagues

The **Rankings** tab is where XP becomes standing. Boards across the
top slice the competition:

**Classroom · Courses · Subjects · Inter-Schools · Weekly · Daily**

![The Classroom board: a top-three podium (11,802 / 11,795 / 11,557 XP) and the ranked list below.](/images/learner/rankings.png)
*Every board has the same shape: podium on top, then the full list with XP.*

## Reading a board

- The **podium** shows the top three, XP under each name.
- Below, the **ranked list**: position, learner, XP — scroll to find
  yourself and the learners either side of you.
- Boards differ by **scope** (your classroom vs your courses vs a
  subject vs between schools) and by **clock** (Weekly and Daily reset
  their boards on their periods; the standing boards accumulate).

## Leaving the boards

Do not want to be ranked publicly? **Settings → (your agent) → Agent
network → Show me on leaderboards** turns your public listing off —
your own progress views stay exactly as they were. See
[Battle preferences](/docs/learner/agent-network/battle-preferences).

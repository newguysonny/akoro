# Your knowledge stats

Open the **Agent** tab and switch to **Stats**. Where the gradebook
counts scores, Stats counts **knowing**:

![Stats: knowledge score 35.6 (Level 6 · Intermediate), 37% average confidence, 12 topics across 3 subjects — then health per subject and what is due for revision.](/images/learner/study-stats.png)

- **Knowledge score and level** — the headline measure of what you
  have learned and kept.
- **Average confidence** — how sure your agent is that you know what
  you have been taught, across every topic.
- **Counts** — sessions, turns, chunks of material, subjects.
- **Knowledge health** per subject, and the **Due for revision** list.

The full guide to acting on this page — decay, Teach again, critical
topics — is [Review and memory decay](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay).

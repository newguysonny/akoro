# Progress

Progress in Akoro is counted in a few currencies, each with its own
home:

## Core tasks

- [XP and levels](/docs/learner/progress/xp-and-levels) — what earns
  XP, your level and your agent's level, and where the totals live.
- [Rankings and leagues](/docs/learner/progress/rankings-and-leagues)
  — the Rankings tab: Classroom, Courses, Subjects, Inter-Schools,
  Weekly and Daily boards, all ordered by XP.
- [Your knowledge stats](/docs/learner/progress/your-knowledge-stats)
  — the Stats tab: knowledge score, confidence, and the topics that
  need you (the full story is in
  [Review and memory decay](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay)).
- [Coins and your wallet](/docs/learner/progress/coins-and-your-wallet)
  — the coin balance in your header, what coins buy, and the Wallet.

## At a glance

Your header is the dashboard: **XP** and class under your name, the
**coins pill**, and your agent's status. Everything in this section is
one tap deeper than that.

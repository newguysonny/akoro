# XP and levels

**XP** (experience points) is Akoro's progress currency. You earn it
across the app:

- **Sessions** — studying with tutors and your agent.
- **Battles** — a daily battle pays **+100 XP** to the winner (see
  [Entry rules and costs](/docs/learner/battles/entry-rules-and-costs)).
- **Practice and assessments** — the work itself, marked.

## Two levels, one partnership

- **Your level** rides on your XP total — it is the number under your
  name in the header (*547 XP · JSS1* pairs your XP with your class).
- **Your agent's level** is tracked on its settings card: *Agent
  Level 2 — 23 turns · 547 XP*, with the road to the next level
  (*Level 2 → 3 · 80% remaining*). Your agent levels up on the same
  work — topics taught, sessions done, turns taken — because it learns
  as you teach it.

## Where the numbers live

- The **header**: your XP, always visible.
- **Settings → (your agent)**: the agent card with its level progress
  and the six stat tiles — topics taught, average confidence, sessions
  done, total turns, XP earned, agent level.
- The **[Stats tab](/docs/learner/progress/your-knowledge-stats)**:
  the knowledge side of the same numbers.

# Review and memory decay

Open the **Stats** tab on the Agent screen. This is your concept
tracker: what you know, how well, and what is fading.

![Stats: knowledge score and level, average confidence, sessions and turns — then knowledge health per subject and the topics due for revision.](/images/learner/study-stats.png)
*Stats: the numbers at the top; below them, the topics that need attention, worst first.*

## What the Stats tab shows

- **Knowledge score and level**, **average confidence**, and your
  counts: sessions, turns, chunks (of material learned), subjects.
- **Knowledge health** per subject — how many topics need attention,
  how many are **critical**, and when you last touched the subject.
- **Due for revision** — the topics whose confidence is decaying:
  *"Teach these again to reinforce before they fade."*

## Memory decay, in plain terms

Confidence is not permanent. A topic you nailed in October fades by
December if nothing revisits it — your agent models that fade, drops the
topic's confidence as time passes, and puts it in **Due for revision**
before it is gone. Being quizzed, practising, or teaching the topic back
restores it.

## Teach again

Each due topic has a **Teach again** button: your agent re-teaches the
topic in the chat and checks you on it — the fastest way to pull a
fading topic back. This is the same teach-back pattern as
[practice](/docs/learner/study/practice), aimed by the decay model at
the topics that need it most.

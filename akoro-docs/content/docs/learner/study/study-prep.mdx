# Study Prep

Study Prep is a guided flow for preparing a topic properly, run inside
your [agent chat](/docs/learner/agent-chat). Ask your agent to prepare
you on a topic — *"Prepare me on fractions"* — and Study Prep starts.

## How it runs

- The flow is **pinned above the messages** while it runs, showing
  *Study Prep · N steps* so you always know where you are.
- **Your guide asks, your agent answers**: each step poses the next
  question or sub-topic, works it with you, and checks understanding
  before moving on.
- Steps you have finished stay in the chat — the flow notes *continued
  further down* when a long preparation spans many messages, so you can
  scroll back through the whole preparation later.
- What Study Prep teaches lands in your knowledge record: confidence on
  the topic moves, and the topic joins your
  [review cycle](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay).

## Study Prep vs a normal chat

A normal chat answers what you ask. Study Prep has a plan: it decides
the order, covers the topic end to end, and does not stop at the first
answer. Use it when you want the whole topic, not a quick explanation —
and use [Exam Prep](/docs/learner/study/exam-prep) when the target is a
specific exam.

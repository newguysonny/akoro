# Exam Prep

Exam Prep is preparation aimed at a real exam — WAEC, NECO or JAMB. It
runs through your agent, on your exam subjects and syllabus, and it is
built on **past questions**: the practise your agent sets is modelled on
real exam papers for your subjects.

## How to use it

1. **Tell your agent the target** — the exam, the subjects, and the
   date, if it does not already know them from your study profile.
2. **Work the practise tasks it sets** — *Practise \<topic\> — 10
   questions* tasks appear in your [Tasks tab](/docs/learner/agent-chat/follow-ups-and-reminders),
   each with the confidence it is based on and a due date.
3. **Teach back what you missed.** The pattern on every task: the agent
   quizzes you, then you explain the ones you got wrong back to it —
   that teach-back is what moves a topic from shaky to solid.
4. **Watch confidence, not just scores.** Your
   [Stats](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay) show confidence
   per topic and flag the critical ones — Exam Prep aims at those first.

## Timed practice

Ask for exam conditions — *"Time me: 20 questions, 30 minutes"* — and
the agent runs the set against the clock, then reviews every miss with
you. Battles are the competitive version of the same muscle: see
[Battles](/docs/learner/battles).

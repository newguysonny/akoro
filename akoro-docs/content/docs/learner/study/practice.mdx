# Practice

Practice is how topics move from *taught* to *known*. It reaches you
three ways:

## Practise tasks

Your agent sets tasks like *Practise Parts of speech (English) — 10
questions*, each carrying:

- the **confidence** it is based on (*"18% confidence. Ask me to quiz
  you on Parts of speech, then explain any you missed back to me."*);
- a **priority** and a **due date** — overdue tasks are marked, and your
  agent will propose spreading them (see the
  [Needs you card](/docs/learner/home/the-needs-you-card)).

Open one from the [Tasks tab](/docs/learner/agent-chat/follow-ups-and-reminders)
and work it in the chat it opens.

## Quiz yourself any time

Tap a suggestion chip — *Quiz me on Algebra* — or just ask. The agent
quizzes from what you have been taught and your past questions, one
question at a time, and marks as you go.

## Teach-back

The step that makes practice stick: after a quiz, **you explain the
missed ones back to the agent**. It listens for the rule, not the
answer — a topic only counts as recovered when your explanation is
right. Teach-back is also what the **Teach again** buttons in your
[Stats](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay) start.

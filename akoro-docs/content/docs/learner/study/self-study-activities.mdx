# Self-study activities

Open **Activity** from the app's side menu for the work your instructors
have set you: assignments, projects, multi-stage work and self-study
activities.

![The Activity list: type chips, Pending / Awaiting / Graded, and a self-study activity that is Not started.](/images/learner/study-activities.png)
*Filters across the top; the card shows the subject, the topic, who set it, and its state.*

## The states

- **Pending** — waiting for you. A card shows *Not started* (or in
  progress), the subject and topic, who set it, and the due date if it
  has one.
- **Awaiting** — you have handed it in; it is with your instructor.
- **Graded** — the score is in; it also appears in your
  [gradebook](/docs/learner/study/your-gradebook).

## Do one, hand it in

Tap a pending activity to open it, do the work it asks for, and submit
from inside it. The card's state changes to Awaiting; when the grade
lands, the activity moves to Graded and the gradebook picks up the
score.

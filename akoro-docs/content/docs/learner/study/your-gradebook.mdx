# Your gradebook

Open **Gradebook** from the app's side menu. It is the record of your
assessed work: every assignment and activity, its score, and your
standing at a glance.

![The gradebook: 2 of 3 submitted, average 68%, and the scored list.](/images/learner/gradebook.png)
*The header pill is your submission rate; the cards below are your average, submitted and missed counts.*

## Reading it

- **Average** — your mean score across graded work.
- **Submitted** — how much of your set work is in (*2 of 3*).
- **Missed** — work that never arrived; missed work counts here even
  when it has no score.
- Each row: the assignment, its type and date, and its **score**. A dash
  means set but not yet graded — self-study activities show a dash until
  they are marked.

## Filters

- **All subjects** — narrow to one subject.
- **All types** — assignments, self-study, and the other work types.
- **Date sort** — newest or oldest first.

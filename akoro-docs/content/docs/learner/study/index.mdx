# Study

Everything structured about learning lives here: the sessions on your
calendar, the preparation flows your agent runs with you, practice and
assessments, the activities your instructors set, and the record of how
it all went — your gradebook.

## Core tasks

- [Teaching sessions](/docs/learner/study/teaching-sessions) — your
  session list, the session sheet, Join Live, and minimising a session
  to finish later.
- [Study Prep](/docs/learner/study/study-prep) — a guided, step-by-step
  preparation flow inside your agent chat.
- [Exam Prep](/docs/learner/study/exam-prep) — WAEC, NECO and JAMB
  preparation with past questions and timed practice.
- [Practice](/docs/learner/study/practice) — the practise tasks your
  agent sets, and quizzing yourself on any topic.
- [The assessment room](/docs/learner/study/the-assessment-room) —
  taking a live assessment from a session.
- [Self-study activities](/docs/learner/study/self-study-activities) —
  work your instructors set in the Activity list: do it, hand it in,
  get graded.
- [Your gradebook](/docs/learner/study/your-gradebook) — every score,
  your average, and what you have missed.
- [Review and memory decay](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay)
  — confidence per topic, what is fading, and teaching it again.

## Where study shows up

- **Learn → My Sessions** for the calendar of sessions.
- **The Agent tab** for Study Prep, practice and your Stats — your
  knowledge score and the topics that need attention.
- **Activity and Gradebook** from the app's side menu for set work and
  scores.

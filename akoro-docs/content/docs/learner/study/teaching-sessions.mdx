# Teaching sessions

**Learn → My Sessions** is your study calendar: today's sessions first,
then the coming days. Filter chips split the list — **All**, **Live
Classes**, **Agent** — with a count on each.

![My Sessions: daily live sessions with subject, time, topics, revisions and reviews.](/images/learner/sessions-list.png)
*Each row: the session, its subject and time, and what it holds — topics, revisions, reviews.*

## The session sheet

Tap a session to open its sheet: the tutor, the level, the subject, and
a description of what the session covers.

![A session sheet: tutor, subject, description — Call Tutor, Message, Take live assessment, Join Live.](/images/learner/session-sheet.png)
*An upcoming live session. Join Live activates when the class is on.*

- **Join Live** — enter the live class when it starts. (You will also
  get a [notification](/docs/learner/home/notifications) when it goes
  live.)
- **Call Tutor / Message** — reach the instructor directly.
- **Take live assessment** — when the session carries one, this opens
  the [assessment room](/docs/learner/study/the-assessment-room).
- **Minimise (−)** — shrink the sheet and continue later without losing
  your place; **Close** dismisses it.

## Agent sessions

Sessions with your agent (the **Agent** filter) run in the
[agent chat](/docs/learner/agent-chat): your agent teaches the topic,
quizzes you, and records how confident you are — which feeds your
[Stats and review](/docs/learner/study/review-and-memory-decay).

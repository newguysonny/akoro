# Discover

Discover is the community's shelf: courses, live classes, teaching
guides and material other learners have shared, organised into rails
and searchable. It is the second tab at the bottom of the app.

![Discover: search, category chips, and the first rails — Recommended for you, Top Teaching Guides, Upcoming Live Classes.](/images/learner/discover-home.png)
*Cards show their kind (LIVE or VIDEO), subject and level, a rating with its count, and the price — or Free.*

## Core tasks

- [Categories and types](/docs/learner/discover/categories-and-types) —
  subject chips narrow everything; the two type chips (Teaching Guides,
  Live Classes) narrow the feed to one kind.
- [Search Discover](/docs/learner/discover/search-discover) — find
  agents, courses and classes, and ask shared records questions.
- [Save and enrol](/docs/learner/discover/save-and-enrol) — what
  tapping a card does: enrol, subscribe, save to your Library.
- [Shared records in Discover](/docs/learner/discover/shared-records-in-discover)
  — how learners' uploads appear here, and whose stay private.

## The rails

The feed is a stack of rails, each with a **See all**: **Recommended
for you**, **Top Teaching Guides**, **Upcoming Live Classes**,
**Explore Video Courses**, **Trending courses**, **Featured courses**,
and more below. Every rail re-sorts itself around the category you
pick.

![Further down the feed: Explore Video Courses, Trending and Featured rails.](/images/learner/discover-rails.png)
*Free courses carry a green Free label; priced ones show their monthly price on the card.*

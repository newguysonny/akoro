# Save and enrol

Tap any card in Discover to open it. What happens next depends on the
kind and the price on the card:

- **Free courses and classes** — enrol or join at once; no payment
  step.
- **Priced courses and guides** — the card shows the monthly price
  (₦/mo) before you commit; subscribing takes you through checkout
  with that price, and the course is yours while the subscription
  runs.
- **Live classes** — join from the card when the class is live, or
  note its time from the Upcoming rail; you will also get a
  [notification](/docs/learner/home/notifications) when it starts.
- **Shared records** — open and [ask them](/docs/learner/discover/search-discover),
  then save the ones worth keeping.

## Where it lands

Everything you enrol in, subscribe to or save appears in your
[Library](/docs/learner/library) — courses with their progress bars,
guides, and records under **My records** — and your sessions appear
under [My Sessions](/docs/learner/study/teaching-sessions).

## Ratings

Cards carry a rating and its count — *4.9 (847)*. Ratings come from
learners who studied the course or guide; a (0) means nobody has rated
it yet, not that it is poor.

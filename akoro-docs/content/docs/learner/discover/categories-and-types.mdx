# Categories and types

Under the search box sits the **Categories** row: **All Topics**, your
subjects (Maths, English, Science…), and two special chips —
**Teaching Guides** and **Live Classes**.

## Subject chips narrow everything

Tap a subject and every rail in the feed — Recommended, Trending,
Featured, all of them — re-sorts to that subject. Tap **All Topics**
to go back to the full feed.

## Type chips narrow to one kind

**Teaching Guides** and **Live Classes** are not subjects; they are
kinds. Selecting one collapses the whole feed to that single kind,
shown as a grid — just the guides, or just the live classes. Selecting
any subject chip restores the full multi-rail feed.

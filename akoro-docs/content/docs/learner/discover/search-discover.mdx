# Search Discover

The search box at the top of Discover searches the whole community
shelf: teaching agents and guides, courses, live classes — and
**records other learners have shared**, matched on the text inside
them, not just their titles.

## Ask a shared record

Open a shared record from the results and you can **ask it questions**
the way you would in the [Reader](/docs/learner/reader/ask-in-the-reader) —
answers grounded in that document, with citations — before deciding
whether it is worth a place in your Library. If it is, save it from
the same place.

## Search vs your Library search

- **Discover search** looks outward: everything public or shared.
- **[Library search](/docs/learner/library/search-inside-your-records)**
  looks inward: only your records, including the private ones nobody
  else can see.

The same document can be in both — yours in your Library, and (if it
is public or shared) everyone's in Discover.

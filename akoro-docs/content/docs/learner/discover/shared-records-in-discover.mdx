# Shared records in Discover

Discover is not only courses and classes — it is also where learners'
own uploads meet the community. Which uploads appear follows the same
rule as everywhere else in Akoro:

- **Free accounts' uploads are public** — they can appear in Discover
  and in search, and other learners can open and ask them.
- **Premium uploads are private** — they appear in Discover **only**
  when the owner turns on **Share in Discover** on that record. An
  unshared Premium record is invisible here: not in the feed, not in
  search, not askable.

The full model — and how to change your mind, either way, with
immediate effect — is in
[Who can see your uploads](/docs/learner/library/who-can-see-your-uploads).

## What you can do with a shared record

Open it, read it in the [Reader](/docs/learner/reader), ask it
questions, and save your own copy to your Library. Saving a copy does
not change the original's visibility — the owner's setting still
rules the original.

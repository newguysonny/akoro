## Assessment Review

**Assessments** covers proctored exam integrity and results. The review
queue gathers sittings that need a human look, with **Flagged** and
**Terminated** views for the exams the proctoring system stopped or
marked. Work through the queue to clear flags and confirm results
before they stand.

![Assessment Review: the proctored-exam review queue](/images/school/learning/assessment-review.png)
*Flagged and terminated sittings wait here for a decision.*

## Review Generated Lessons

**Review AI Lessons** is where AI-built lesson plans are approved or
corrected. The queue is split into **Pending**, **Approved**, and
**Rejected**; open a plan to read it, fix anything, and approve it.
Students are never blocked while you review — lessons keep flowing, and
your corrections shape what the agent produces next.

![Review Generated Lessons, with its Pending, Approved, and Rejected tabs](/images/school/learning/lesson-review.png)
*Approve or correct AI-built plans without holding up teaching.*

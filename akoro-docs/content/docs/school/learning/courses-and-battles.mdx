## Courses

**Courses** manages your live and on-demand courses. The header actions
cover the three ways teaching starts:

- **Schedule Session** — add a session to a course.
- **New On-Demand** — build a video course learners take in their own
  time.
- **New Live Class** — create a live course taught in scheduled
  sessions.

Below, **Your Courses** lists every course with its kind (Live Course or
On-Demand), status, and student count. Each card opens its **Curriculum**
(live courses) or **Manage** view (on-demand), and carries share, edit,
and delete actions. A **Live Sessions** tab gathers the sessions across
your courses.

## Battles

**Battles** runs quiz battles across your school. Counters at the top
track Total, Live Now, Scheduled, and Completed battles, with matching
filters — All, Live, Scheduled, Lobby, Completed. **New Battle** creates
one; learners join from their apps and compete live.

## Tasks

**Tasks** schedules work for your school's agent — once, or on a repeat.
Counters track Pending, Completed, and Overdue tasks, with All, Pending,
Scheduled, and Done filters. **New Task** describes the work and when it
should run; the agent carries it out and reports back in its chat.

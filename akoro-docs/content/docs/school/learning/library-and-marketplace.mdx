## Content Library

The **Library** holds everything your agent has created. Filter by
kind — All, Lesson Plans, Worksheets, Reports, Assessments, Attendance,
Interactive — and open any item to reuse it. When the library is empty,
ask your agent to create a lesson plan, worksheet, or report in
[AI Chat](/docs/school/learning/ai-chat-and-self-study) and it appears
here.

![The Content Library and its kind filters](/images/school/learning/content-library.png)
*Every agent creation, filterable by kind.*

## Tools & Assets

**Tools & Assets** (the marketplace) lists your unlocked tools, assets,
and templates. Teaching tools include the **Podcast Generator** (turn a
topic into a two-host audio episode with a transcript), **Common
Misconceptions** (the wrong ideas students arrive with, and how to fix
each one), **Real-World Connections** (everyday contexts and careers
built on a topic), and **Lesson Hooks** (five-minute openers that spark
curiosity). Purchases are recorded under
[Tool Purchases](/docs/school/settings/billing) in Settings.

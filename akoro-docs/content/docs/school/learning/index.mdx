Alongside the ERP, the portal carries Akoro's learning surface — the
same courses, live classes, and AI agent your teachers and learners use,
seen from the school's seat.

- [Courses, battles & tasks](/docs/school/learning/courses-and-battles) —
  the course catalogue, quiz battles, and work scheduled for your agent.
- [AI chat & self study](/docs/school/learning/ai-chat-and-self-study) —
  talk to your school's agent, and assign AI-guided topics to learners.
- [Assessments & lesson review](/docs/school/learning/assessments-and-lesson-review) —
  proctored-exam integrity and approving AI-built lesson plans.
- [Library & marketplace](/docs/school/learning/library-and-marketplace) —
  everything your agent creates, and the tools you can unlock.
- [Wallet & content](/docs/school/learning/wallet-and-content) — the
  coin balance every AI feature draws from, and on-brand content
  generation.

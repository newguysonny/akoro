## Wallet & AI Credits

Every AI feature in the portal — the agent, generators, self study —
draws from one school coin balance. **Wallet & AI Credits** shows the
balance and sells top-up packs of 500, 2,000, 5,000, and 20,000 coins:
pick a pack and **Continue to checkout**. You can also top up with a
voucher code. **Recent activity** lists the wallet's transactions. The
same balance appears on the dashboard — tap its card there to jump
straight here.

![Wallet & AI Credits: balance, top-up packs, and recent activity](/images/school/learning/wallet.png)
*One balance powers every AI feature in the portal.*

## Content

**Content** generates on-brand material and manages your channels.
Generators cover **Blog** posts, **Social Posts**, and **Activities**;
under Channels & Settings sit **External Channels** and **Content
Settings**, plus a Quick Guide.

Posts are written in your school's brand voice and tone from Content
Settings — set those once for a consistent style. Generated blog posts
can be copied straight into your [website builder](/docs/school/website-builder/build)
or newsletter.

## Agent Chat

**AI Chat** is a conversation with your school's own agent. The header
shows the agent's name and level, with **Edit** (its identity) and
**Train** (how it should answer) beside it. Template chips start common
jobs in one tap:

- **Lesson Plan**, **Worksheets**, and **Report Writing** template fills
- **Grading** and **Attendance / Roll Call**
- **Interactive Material** and **Exam Prep Assessment**
- **Quiz Generator** and **Create Battle**
- **Student Progress** and **Reports & Battle Report**
- **Send Reminder** and **Schedule Session**

Anything the agent creates — lesson plans, worksheets, reports — is
saved to your [Content Library](/docs/school/learning/library-and-marketplace).

## AI Self Study

**AI Self Study** assigns a topic for learners across your school to
work through with their own AI agent. Counters track Assignments,
Learners assigned, In progress, and Completed.

To assign one: pick a topic, let the AI draft the subtopics and
objectives, edit them, then send it to one learner or the whole class.
Each learner works through it with their own agent, at their own pace,
while the counters here follow completion.

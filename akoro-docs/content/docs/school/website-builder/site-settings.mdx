## School Website

**School Website** publishes your site and jumps into the builder,
with shortcuts to Appearance, Templates, and **Preview Website**.
**Enable School Website** is the master switch: when it is on,
visitors to your domain see your public website instead of the login
page. The switch saves instantly — there is no save button to press.

With **Sync with School Settings** on, your website's identity —
school name, logo, address, phone, principal, and contact email —
always follows Settings → School Profile: change it there and the
website updates automatically. While sync is on, the hero and browser
titles use your school name, and custom titles set in Appearance are
ignored.

## Templates

**Templates** lists the website templates installed for your school.
A template can be customised and activated; activating a sample
template can import its pages and menu, so a new site does not start
from a blank page. **Add New** installs another template, **Add New
Template** creates one, and the page can switch to Theme mode.

## AI Website Sync

**AI Website Sync** rewrites your site from your school's own
information, one page at a time. Pick a website template or add a
page first, then **Start AI sync**. Syncing is charged to the school
wallet per page, based on actual usage — the same as every other AI
feature.

The page lists any missing information before you start. The AI only
writes from facts you have given it, so anything missing — a street
address, city, state, phone number, email address, or the year the
school was established — is simply left out of the site. Each gap has
an **Add** action, so you can fill it in and sync a fuller site.

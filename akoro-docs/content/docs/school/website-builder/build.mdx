The website builder edits the public site families see — the pages
they read, the menus they follow, and the look of the home page.

## Pages

**Pages** lists the content pages on your school website, with each
page's slug, status, and order. Filter by All, Published, Draft, or
Trash, and use **Add New Page** to create one.

## Menus

**Menus** builds the navigation for your website — the primary menu,
the footer menu, and social links. **Create Menu** starts one; select
a menu to edit what it points to.

## Media Library

The **Media Library** holds the images, videos, and files to use on
your website, filterable by type, with a Trash for removed items.
**Upload Files** adds media from your device — drag and drop works
too — and **Add by URL** brings in a file hosted elsewhere. Each item
lists its type and URL, ready to place on a page.

## Appearance

**Appearance** designs your website's home page and global look, with
a live preview as you work. The hero section takes a title (leave it
blank to use the school name) and a subtitle, followed by the About
section. SEO settings set the meta title, keywords, and description,
and tracking scripts can be added to the header and footer — analytics
and chat widgets included. The colour theme sets primary, secondary,
and accent colours and a background mode (Light, Dark, or Auto), and
home-page sections can be hidden by unchecking them. **Save
Appearance** applies the design.

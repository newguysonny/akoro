## All Transactions

**All Transactions** is every fee payment recorded, filterable by
method — Cash, Bank, Online, POS, or Cheque. If a payment was taken,
it is here.

## Online transactions and payments

**Online Transactions** lists payments made online. **Online
Payments** is the checkout view: Paystack checkouts started from your
public **Pay Fees** and **Apply** pages, filterable by status
(Success, Pending, Failed) and purpose (Fees or Admission) — so a
parent who says "I paid online" can be matched to a checkout, even a
failed one.

![Online Payments: status and purpose filters](/images/school/fees/online-payments.png)
*Every checkout from the public pages, successful or not.*

## Fee Challans

**Fee Challans** are bank payment challans: each has a status —
Unpaid, Paid, or Cancelled — and **New Challan** issues one for a
student to pay at the bank.

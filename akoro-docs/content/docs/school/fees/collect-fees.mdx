## Fees Dashboard

The **Fees Dashboard** puts collections at a glance: Total Collected,
Outstanding, Total Assigned, Collected Today, and counts of
transactions, online transactions, and fee types.

![Fees Dashboard: collected, outstanding, and assigned](/images/school/fees/dashboard-stats.png)
*The money picture, before you chase anyone.*

## Collect Fees

**Collect Fees** records a fee payment: select the student and record
what they paid against their assigned fees. The payment lands in the
student's ledger and in [All Transactions](/docs/school/fees/transactions).

## Search Due Fees

**Search Due Fees** finds students with outstanding balances, by
class — the list to work from when following up on dues.

## Due slips

**Generate Due Slip** creates dues notices for a class: pick the class
and a period label, and a slip is created for every student in the
class with an outstanding balance. **Due Slip History** keeps the
slips already generated.

![Generate Due Slip: one notice per student who owes](/images/school/fees/due-slip.png)
*A slip for every student in the class with a balance.*

The Fees & Accounts section is the school's money: what is charged,
who owes it, what has been collected, and the wider accounts beyond
fees.

- [Fee structure](/docs/school/fees/fee-structure) — fee types,
  groups, discounts, assignment, and carrying balances forward.
- [Collect fees](/docs/school/fees/collect-fees) — the fees dashboard,
  recording payments, finding dues, and issuing due slips.
- [Transactions](/docs/school/fees/transactions) — every payment,
  online checkouts, and bank challans.
- [Accounts](/docs/school/fees/accounts) — income, expenses, heads,
  and bank accounts.

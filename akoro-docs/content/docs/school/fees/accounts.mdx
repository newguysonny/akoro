## Accounts Dashboard

The **Accounts Dashboard** covers money beyond fees: Total Income,
Total Expenses, Net Balance, counts of income and expense entries,
and the number of bank accounts.

![Accounts Dashboard: income, expenses, net balance](/images/school/fees/accounts-dashboard.png)
*The whole school's money, not just fees.*

## Income and expenses

**Income** records money received outside fee collection, and
**Expense** records money paid out. Each entry sits under a head:
**Income Heads** and **Expense Heads** are the categories (maintained
with **New Head**), and both lists filter by head.

## Bank Accounts

**Bank Accounts** holds the school's bank accounts — the accounts
challans and transfers reference. **New Account** adds one.

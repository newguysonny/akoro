## Fee Types

**Fee Types** are the individual chargeable items — each thing a
student can be charged for. **New Fee Type** adds one. Everything else
in fees is built from these items.

## Fee Groups

**Fee Groups** bundle related fee types into one charge — for example
a "School Fees" group for termly fees. **New Group** creates one.
Groups are what you assign, instead of itemising every fee each time.

## Fees Discount

**Fees Discount** holds scholarships and waivers. **New Discount**
creates one, to be applied against a student's charges.

## Assign Fees

**Assign Fees** attaches a fee to a class — or to a specific student.
Assigned fees are what appear as dues in a student's ledger and in
[Search Due Fees](/docs/school/fees/collect-fees).

## Fees Carry Forward

**Fees Carry Forward** moves unpaid balances into a new session: pick
the from-session and the to-session and carry forward, so last term's
debt follows the student instead of being written off. The page keeps
the history of carry-forwards already done.

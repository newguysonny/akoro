## Hostel Dashboard

The **Hostel Dashboard** sums up boarding at a glance: hostels,
rooms, total bed spaces, students allocated, and room types.

![Hostel Dashboard: boarding facilities at a glance](/images/school/transport-hostel/hostel-dashboard.png)
*Beds, rooms, and how many students are placed.*

## Manage Hostels

**Manage Hostels** lists the boarding houses. **New Hostel** adds
one; rooms and allocations hang off the hostels defined here.

## Room Types

**Room Types** are the room categories and their pricing. **New
Type** adds one, so rooms of the same kind share a price instead of
being set one by one.

## Manage Rooms

**Manage Rooms** holds the rooms per hostel: filter by hostel to see
one house's rooms, and add with **New Room**. A room's type comes from
Room Types, and its beds count towards the dashboard's bed spaces.

## Student Allocation

**Student Allocation** is who sleeps where. **Allocate Student**
places a student in a room; allocations carry a status of Active or
Vacated, so a student who leaves boarding frees the bed without
losing the record. Allocated students count on the dashboard, and the
students themselves are the school's boarders from the
[Student List](/docs/school/students/student-list-and-classes).

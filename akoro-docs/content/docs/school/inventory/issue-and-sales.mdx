## Issue Item

**Issue Item** gives stock out to staff and departments: pick the
item, enter the quantity, say who it is issued to and what it is for.
Every issue is recorded with its item, quantity, recipient, purpose,
and date, and the store's in-stock count drops accordingly — this is
how stock that is used, rather than sold, leaves the shelf. Stock
levels live on the [Item List](/docs/school/inventory/stock).

## Point of Sale

**Point of Sale** sells items — uniforms, books, and anything else
the store sells. Pick the item, set the quantity and unit price, name
the buyer, and **Record Sale**. The sale total is worked out for you,
and the item's stock goes down by the quantity sold.

## Sales History

**Sales History** is every POS sale in one list, so the store's
takings can be checked against the POS Sales figure on the
[Inventory Dashboard](/docs/school/inventory/stock).

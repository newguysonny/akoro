## Inventory Dashboard

The **Inventory Dashboard** covers the store at a glance: items,
low-stock count, stock value, POS sales, suppliers, and open purchase
orders — the figures the rest of this section maintains.

![Inventory Dashboard: stock, sales, and suppliers](/images/school/inventory/dashboard.png)
*What the store holds, what it is worth, and what is on order.*

## Item List

The **Item List** is everything in the store: each item's category,
how many are in stock, the reorder level it should not fall below,
and its unit price. Filter by category, and register something new
with **New Item**. When stock falls to the reorder level, the item
counts towards the dashboard's low-stock figure.

![Item List: stock levels, reorder points, and unit prices](/images/school/inventory/item-list.png)
*Every item, with the level that triggers a reorder.*

## Add Stock

**Add Stock** receives stock into the store: pick the item, enter the
quantity and unit cost, name the supplier, and add a note if needed.
Each receipt is listed with its quantity, cost, supplier, and date,
and the item's in-stock count goes up by what arrived.

## Item Categories

**Item Categories** group store items — Cleaning, Stationery,
Uniforms, and your own under **New Category**. Categories drive the
Item List filter.

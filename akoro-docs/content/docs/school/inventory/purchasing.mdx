## Suppliers

**Suppliers** is who you buy from — each with a phone number and
email. **New Supplier** adds one. Suppliers are picked when receiving
stock and when raising purchase orders, so keep the list current.

## Purchase Orders

**Purchase Orders** are the orders placed with suppliers. Each order
moves through a status — Draft, Ordered, Received, or Cancelled —
and **New Order** raises one. Open purchase orders count on the
[Inventory Dashboard](/docs/school/inventory/stock).

## Goods Receipts

**Goods Receipts** records deliveries against purchase orders:
**New Receipt** books in what actually arrived, which is what stock
receipts and the order's Received status are checked against.

## Supplier Payments

**Supplier Payments** records money paid to suppliers, with **New
Payment** for each one — the purchasing side of the school's money,
alongside the wider [Accounts](/docs/school/fees/accounts).

## Core Competencies

**Core Competencies** are the cross-cutting skills CBC runs through
every subject. **New Competency** adds one to the school's set, and
assessments can then rate learners against them as well as against
subject outcomes.

## Pathways & Tracks

**Pathways & Tracks** defines the senior-school pathways students
move into. **New Pathway** adds one, so learners can be tracked
against the pathway they are headed for.

## CBC Reports

**CBC Reports** shows the competency-level distribution across the
school: how many assessments sit at Exceeding, Meeting, Approaching,
and Below Expectations, and the total assessments recorded — the
at-a-glance picture of how the cohort is doing under CBC.

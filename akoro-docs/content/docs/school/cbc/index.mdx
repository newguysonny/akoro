The CBC section supports schools running Kenya's competency-based
curriculum. The **CBC Dashboard** gives the overview; assessment works
in competency levels rather than marks.

## Strands & Outcomes

**Strands & Outcomes** holds the curriculum structure: strands and
their learning outcomes, organised by subject. **New Strand** adds one.
Assessments rate students against these outcomes.

## CBC Assessments

**CBC Assessments** records competency ratings per student. Each
rating is one of the four CBC levels:

- **Exceeding Expectations**
- **Meeting Expectations**
- **Approaching Expectations**
- **Below Expectations**

Filter the list by level, and record a rating with **New Assessment**.

![CBC Assessments: the four competency levels as filters](/images/school/cbc/assessments.png)
*Ratings are levels, not scores.*

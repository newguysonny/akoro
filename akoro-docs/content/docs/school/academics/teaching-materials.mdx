---
title: Teaching materials
description: Resources and materials organised per class, section, and subject.
---

**Teaching Materials** gathers the resources and materials for teaching,
organised the way teaching is: pick a class, narrow to a section (or
all sections), and pick a subject to see its materials.

Keep materials here — rather than scattered across drives and chats —
and every teacher of that subject in that class works from the same
set. Materials here complement what teachers and the agent create in
the [Content Library](/docs/school/learning/library-and-marketplace).

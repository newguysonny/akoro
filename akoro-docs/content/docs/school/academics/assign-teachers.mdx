## Assign Teacher to Subjects

**Assign Teacher to Subjects** sets the teaching load: choose a class,
and the page lists its subjects so you can set the teacher for each
one. A subject with no teacher here is a gap the timetable and lesson
planning will trip over — this page is where gaps get closed.

![Assign Teacher to Subjects starts by choosing a class](/images/school/academics/assign-teacher.png)
*Pick a class, then set the teacher for each of its subjects.*

## Assign Class Teacher

**Assign Class Teacher** picks each class's form teacher from your
staff: choose a class, optionally narrow to a section (or leave it as
the whole class), and pick the teacher. The class teacher anchors the
class's pastoral care, attendance, and report-card remarks.

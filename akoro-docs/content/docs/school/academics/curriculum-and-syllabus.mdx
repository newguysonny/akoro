## Curriculum

**Curriculum** records the frameworks the school has adopted, filtered
by level and status. **New Curriculum** registers one, so every other
academic tool can refer to the framework actually being taught.

## Syllabus

**Syllabus** is the outline per subject: pick a subject and class to
see its syllabus, with active and inactive entries. **New Syllabus**
adds an outline. This is the reference the scheme of work and lesson
plans break down into teachable weeks.

## Scheme of Work

The **Scheme of Work** is the termly, weekly coverage plan per class:
rows of class, subject, term, week, and topic, filtered by class,
subject, and term (First, Second, Third). **New Scheme** adds coverage.
Teachers plan lessons against it, and the
[lesson planner](/docs/school/lesson-planning/lesson-plans) tracks how much of it
has actually been covered.

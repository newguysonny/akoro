title: Academics
description: The school's academic structure — sessions, calendar, levels, classes, subjects, teachers, curriculum, and timetables.
---

The Academics section builds the structure everything else hangs on:
sessions and terms, levels and classes, subjects and who teaches them,
the curriculum being followed, and the timetable. Its dashboard sums it
up — sessions, classes, subjects, and timetable at a glance.

Set it up in order: [sessions and levels](/docs/school/academics/setup)
first, then [classes and subjects](/docs/school/academics/classes-and-subjects),
[teachers](/docs/school/academics/assign-teachers), and
[timetables](/docs/school/academics/timetables).

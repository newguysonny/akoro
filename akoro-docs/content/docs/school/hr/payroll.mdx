title: Payroll

---

## Payroll

**Payroll** runs salaries monthly: open a month and **Generate
Payroll** to build that month's run from the salaries on record.
Pending payroll shows on the HR Dashboard until a run is generated.

## Set Salary and templates

**Set Salary** updates individual staff monthly salaries.
**Salary Templates** holds reusable salary structures (**New
Template**), so a whole grade of staff can share one structure instead
of being priced one by one.

## Staff loans

**Manage Staff Loans** records loans to staff and their deductions
(**New Loan**). Active loans count on the HR Dashboard, and deductions
run against payroll until a loan is cleared.

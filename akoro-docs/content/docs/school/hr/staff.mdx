## Staff Directory

The **Staff Directory** is everyone: staff number, name, department,
designation, phone, and status, filterable by department. **Add
Staff** registers someone directly.

![Staff Directory: staff number, department, designation, status](/images/school/hr/staff-directory.png)
*The whole staff list in one place.*

## Academic and non-academic staff

**Academic Staff** and **Non-Academic Staff** group the same people by
role instead. Academic groups include Leadership, Class Teachers,
Academic Coordinators, Special Needs Teachers, and Teaching
Assistants; non-academic groups run from Administration, Bursary &
Accounts, and Front Desk to Security, Drivers, Kitchen, Maintenance,
Medical, ICT, Library, and Boarding & Welfare.

Pick a role card to open that group's list, and **Add Staff** inside a
group files the new member under it automatically. Add each person's
email so their app login is linked to their staff record.

## Departments and designations

**Departments** are the school's units (Academics, Administration,
and yours under **New Department**); **Designations** are job titles
(Bursar, Registrar, Teacher — **New Designation** adds more). Both
feed the directory's filters and each staff record.

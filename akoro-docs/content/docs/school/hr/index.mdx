The Human Resources section manages the people who run the school.
Its dashboard counts total and active staff, present today,
departments, pending leaves, pending payroll, and active loans.

- [Staff](/docs/school/hr/staff) — the directory, academic and
  non-academic role groups, departments, and designations.
- [Teacher requests](/docs/school/hr/teacher-requests) — teachers who
  asked to join your school.
- [Payroll](/docs/school/hr/payroll) — monthly runs, salaries,
  templates, and staff loans.
- [Leave & appraisals](/docs/school/hr/leave-and-appraisals) — leave
  types, approvals, and performance reviews.
- [ID cards & settings](/docs/school/hr/id-cards-and-settings) —
  printable staff ID cards and the module's preferences.

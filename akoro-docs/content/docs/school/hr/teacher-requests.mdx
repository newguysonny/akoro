---
title: Teacher requests
description: Teachers who asked to join your school — approve them into your staff or reject the request.
---

**Teacher Requests** lists teachers who asked to join your school
from their own Akoro accounts, with the subjects they teach.
**Approve** brings a teacher onto your staff; **Reject** declines the
request.

![Teacher Requests: the teacher, their subjects, Approve or Reject](/images/school/hr/teacher-requests.png)
*Join requests arrive here; nothing joins your staff without approval.*

Approved teachers appear in the [Staff Directory](/docs/school/hr/staff)
and can be given classes and subjects under
[Assign teachers](/docs/school/academics/assign-teachers).

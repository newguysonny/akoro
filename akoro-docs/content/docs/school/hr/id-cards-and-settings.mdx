## Staff ID Card

**Staff ID Card** previews and prints staff identity cards: select a
staff member to see their card as it will print.

## HR Settings

**HR Settings** holds the module's preferences:

- **Work Days Per Week** — used for attendance and payroll
  calculations.
- **Default Annual Leave Days** — the starting allowance for leave.
- **Payroll Day of Month** — the day salaries are due each month.
- **Require Leave Approval** — whether leave requests must be approved
  before they count.

## Leave Types

**Leave Types** are the leave categories and their allowances in days
— Annual Leave, Casual Leave, Sick Leave, and your own under **New
Type**.

![Leave Types: categories with their days allowed](/images/school/hr/leave-types.png)
*Each type carries its own allowance.*

## Approving and managing leave

**Approve Leave** is the pending queue: requests waiting for a
decision. **Leave Management** is every request, filterable by status
(Pending, Approved, Rejected), and where a request can also be raised
directly with **New Request**. Whether approval is required at all is
an [HR setting](/docs/school/hr/id-cards-and-settings).

## Appraisals

Appraisals run in three parts: **Appraisal Cycles** are the review
periods (**New Cycle**), **Appraisal Criteria** are what staff are
scored on (**New Criterion**), and **Appraisals** are the performance
reviews themselves (**New Appraisal**) — one per staff member per
cycle.

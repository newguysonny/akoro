## Lesson Plan Review

**Lesson Plan Review** is the queue of plans awaiting a decision.
Reviewers approve or reject each submitted plan and can leave
feedback; a rejected plan goes back to its teacher with the notes
attached.

## Lesson Plan Approvals

**Lesson Plan Approvals** lists the approved plans — the ones cleared
for teaching.

## Lesson Plan Coverage

**Lesson Plan Coverage** shows how much of the curriculum is planned
and approved: total plans, the approved coverage percentage, what is
still in review, and what is still draft. It is the gap-spotting view
heads of department check against the
[scheme of work](/docs/school/academics/curriculum-and-syllabus).

![Lesson Plan Coverage: approved, in review, and still draft](/images/school/lesson-planning/coverage.png)
*How much of the term is actually planned.*

## Lesson Plan Reports

**Lesson Plan Reports** breaks the pipeline down by status —
approved, rejected, pending review, and drafts — for reporting beyond
the dashboard's headline counts.

## Lesson Planner Settings

**Lesson Planner Settings** holds the workflow preferences: whether
approval is required before teaching, the weekly submission day, and
how many weeks ahead teachers must plan. **Save Settings** applies
them.

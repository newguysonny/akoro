The Lesson Planning section runs the school's plan submission and
approval pipeline: teachers draft plans, submit them for review, and
heads track what has been approved before the week starts. Review,
coverage, and settings are on the
[next page](/docs/school/lesson-planning/review-and-coverage).

## Lesson Planner Dashboard

The **Lesson Planner Dashboard** counts the pipeline: total lesson
plans, and how many are awaiting review, approved, rejected, or still
drafts.

![Lesson Planner Dashboard: plans by pipeline stage](/images/school/lesson-planning/dashboard.png)
*Where every plan in the school currently stands.*

## Lesson Plans

**Lesson Plans** lists all lesson plans, filterable by status —
Draft, Submitted, Approved, or Rejected — and by class. **New Plan**
starts one.

## Lesson Planner Guide

The **Lesson Planner Guide** is a built-in page that explains how the
planning workflow runs, in four steps: teachers draft a plan per
topic or week with objectives, activities, and resources; setting the
status to Submitted sends it to the review queue; a reviewer approves
or rejects it, with feedback attached to rejections; and coverage and
reports then show the plan counts per status so gaps surface before
the week starts.

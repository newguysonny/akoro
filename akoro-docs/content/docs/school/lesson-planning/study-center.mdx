The Study Center is where teaching activity itself is kept — what the
syllabus asks for, what was actually taught, and the materials and
homework around it.

## Study Center Dashboard

The **Study Center Dashboard** gives the overview of teaching and
learning activity across the school.

## Manage Syllabus

**Manage Syllabus** holds topics per class and subject, filterable by
class and by status — Pending or Covered. **New Topic** adds one.
Marking topics covered here is what turns the
[syllabus](/docs/school/academics/curriculum-and-syllabus) into a
coverage record.

## Classwork & Logbook

**Classwork & Logbook** is the daily record of what was taught.
**New Log Entry** records a class's work for the day, so the logbook
can be checked against the syllabus topics above.

## Manage Resources

**Manage Resources** holds teaching and study materials, filterable
by class and by type — Document, Video, or Link. **New Resource** adds
one, filed against its class and subject.

## Homework & Assignments

**Homework & Assignments** sets and tracks homework, filterable by
class and by status — Open or Closed. **New Homework** sets one.

## Live Classes

**Live Classes** lists online sessions run over Zoom or Meet,
filterable by status — Scheduled, Live, or Ended. **Schedule Live
Class** sets one up. These are the school's own online sessions; live
classes attached to courses live in the
[learning hub](/docs/school/learning/courses-and-battles).

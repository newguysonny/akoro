The Meetings section covers the two structured conversations a school
runs: parent–teacher meetings (PTM), on this page, and classroom
observation and supervision — [OSM](/docs/school/meetings/osm).

## PTM Dashboard

The **PTM Dashboard** is the parent–teacher meeting overview: total
meetings, how many are scheduled, attendance records, guardians
attended, and open follow-ups.

![PTM Dashboard: meetings, attendance, and open follow-ups](/images/school/meetings/ptm-dashboard.png)
*Parent engagement at a glance.*

## PTM Schedule Meetings

**PTM Schedule Meetings** plans the meetings, filterable by status —
Scheduled, Completed, or Cancelled. **Schedule Meeting** creates one;
attach a class when the meeting is class-specific.

## PTM Attendance & Remarks

**PTM Attendance & Remarks** records who attended and what was
discussed. **Record Attendance** logs each guardian against a meeting,
with the key points discussed per student.

## PTM Follow-ups

**PTM Follow-ups** holds the actions agreed with parents — extra
lessons, fee discussions, behavioural support — each with a due date,
filterable by status: Open or Done. **New Follow-up** adds one, so
nothing agreed in the room is forgotten.

## PTM Reports

**PTM Reports** shows the engagement metrics behind the dashboard —
how meetings, attendance, and follow-ups are trending.

## PTM Guide

The **PTM Guide** is a built-in page on how to run effective
parent–teacher meetings: schedule early, at least two weeks ahead,
and share the agenda so parents come prepared; record attendance and
remarks during the meeting; and turn anything that needs action into
a follow-up with a due date.

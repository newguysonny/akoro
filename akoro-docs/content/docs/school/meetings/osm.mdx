OSM is the school's classroom observation and supervision cycle: a
teacher is observed, the visit is scored and written up, a second
reviewer moderates the evaluation, and the results aggregate into
reports leadership can track across terms.

## OSM Dashboard

The **OSM Dashboard** gives the observation and supervision overview:
observation sessions, completed sessions, evaluations, evaluations
pending moderation, and the average score.

![OSM Dashboard: sessions, evaluations, moderation, and average score](/images/school/meetings/osm-dashboard.png)
*The observation cycle in five numbers.*

## OSM Sessions

**OSM Sessions** are the classroom observation sessions, filterable
by status — Scheduled or Completed. **New Session** schedules one,
with the teacher, the observer, the date, and a focus area, so the
teacher knows what the visit will look at.

## OSM Evaluate

**OSM Evaluate** scores observed sessions. **New Evaluation** records
one: a score plus written strengths and areas for improvement for the
teacher observed.

## OSM Moderation

**OSM Moderation** lists evaluations awaiting moderation. A second
reviewer checks each pending evaluation for fairness and consistency
before it becomes final.

## OSM Reports

**OSM Reports** shows observation outcomes: the average score, total
evaluations, how many are pending moderation, and sessions completed.

## OSM Guide

The **OSM Guide** is a built-in page describing the observation and
supervision workflow in four steps — schedule the observation,
evaluate the visit, moderate the evaluation, and review the trends in
reports — matching the pages above in order.

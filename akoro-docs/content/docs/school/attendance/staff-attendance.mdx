---
title: Staff attendance
description: The daily staff register, alongside QR check-ins for staff.
---

**Staff Attendance** is the daily staff register: who among the
teaching and non-teaching staff is in, each day. It sits with the
[HR tools](/docs/school/hr), where attendance feeds into staff records
and leave.

Staff also check in through the [QR station](/docs/school/attendance/qr-attendance)
when it is enabled for staff, and those scans appear in the QR
Attendance Report with late flags past your cutoff — so the register
and the gate record can be checked against each other.

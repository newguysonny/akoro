---
title: Attendance
description: Daily class registers, a QR scan-in station for the gate, and the staff register.
---

Attendance runs three ways in the portal, and all three feed the same
records:

- [Mark attendance](/docs/school/attendance/mark-attendance) — the
  class register, marked by hand.
- [QR attendance](/docs/school/attendance/qr-attendance) — a scan-in
  station at the gate for staff and students, with late flagging and a
  full event report.
- [Staff attendance](/docs/school/attendance/staff-attendance) — the
  daily staff register.

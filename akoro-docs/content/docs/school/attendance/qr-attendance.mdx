## The scan station

**QR Attendance** is the scan-in station: it shows a **Station QR
Code** that staff and students scan at the gate to check in and out.
Scanner hardware posts to the same check-in service, so a fixed scanner
and phone scans land in the same record. Anyone missed by the scanner
can be checked in by hand under **Manual Check-in** — pick Staff or
Student and select the person.

![QR Attendance: the station code and manual check-in](/images/school/attendance/qr-station.png)
*One station for gate scans and manual check-ins.*

## Settings

**QR Attendance Setting** configures the station: turn QR attendance
on or off, choose whether it tracks students, staff, or both, and set
**Mark Late After** — check-ins after that time are flagged late in
reports. **Save Settings** applies the change.

![QR Attendance Setting: tracking toggles and the late cutoff](/images/school/attendance/qr-settings.png)
*Set who is tracked and when a check-in counts as late.*

## Report

The **QR Attendance Report** lists every scan-in and scan-out event,
filterable by type (Staff or Student) and direction (In or Out) — the
audit trail behind the day's attendance figures and the late flags.

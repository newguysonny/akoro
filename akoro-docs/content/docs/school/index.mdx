The school portal at [schools.akoro.ng](https://schools.akoro.ng) is where a
school is run day to day. It combines a full school ERP — students,
academics, attendance, exams, fees, HR, front office, library, inventory,
transport, hostel, and assets — with Akoro's learning surface: courses,
live classes, battles, and the AI agent that teaches and answers students.

These guides follow the portal's own navigation, section by section. What
you see in the menu depends on the modules your school has enabled, so
your portal may show fewer sections than these guides cover.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/docs/school/getting-started) — sign in and read the
  dashboard.
- [Students & admissions](/docs/school/students) — admit, place, and track
  every learner.
- [Fees & accounts](/docs/school/fees) — fee structures, collection, and
  the school's books.
- [School settings](/docs/school/settings) — modules, roles, billing, and
  backups.

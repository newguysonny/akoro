The Communications section is how the school talks to its community —
announcements everyone sees, messages sent to a chosen audience, and
the calendar and photos around them. Printable documents live under
[Document templates](/docs/school/communications/document-templates).

## Notice Board

The **Notice Board** holds school-wide announcements, filterable by
audience — All, Students, Staff, or Parents. **New Notice** posts one.

![Notice Board: announcements by audience](/images/school/communications/notice-board.png)
*One board, filtered by who it is for.*

## Compose Broadcast

**Compose Broadcast** sends a message to your community. Give it a
title and the message, pick a channel — In-App, SMS, Email, or
WhatsApp — and an audience: All, Students, Staff, or Parents. Save it
as a Draft to finish later, or mark it Sent.

![Compose Broadcast: title, channel, audience, and message](/images/school/communications/broadcast.png)
*One message, four channels, a chosen audience.*

## Broadcast History

**Broadcast History** lists previously composed messages, filterable
by status — Draft or Sent — so anything the school has said can be
found again.

## Events & Holidays

**Events & Holidays** is the school calendar of events and holidays.
Filter by type — Event or Holiday — and add dates with **New Entry**.

## Image Gallery

The **Image Gallery** holds school photo albums. **Add Image** puts a
photo in.

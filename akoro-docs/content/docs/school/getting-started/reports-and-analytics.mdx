**Reports & Analytics** (under Facilities & Services) is a whole-school
overview with links to every module report. Instead of hunting through
the menu, open it when you want a module's numbers — each card jumps to
that module's own dashboard, where its figures and reports live.

![Module Dashboards: a link to every module's dashboard](/images/school/getting-started/reports-dashboards.png)
*Every module dashboard, one tap away.*

The list covers Student, Academic, Fees, Accounts, HR, Exam, Front
Office, CBC, PTM, Lesson Planner, OSM, Study Center, Library, Inventory,
Transport, Hostel, and Asset dashboards — the same dashboards described
throughout these guides, gathered in one place.

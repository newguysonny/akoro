## Sign in

Open [schools.akoro.ng](https://schools.akoro.ng) and sign in with
**Continue with Google**, or with the email address and password your
school account was created with. The portal is exclusively for schools
and administrators — learners and teachers use their own Akoro apps.

New to Akoro? **Create an account** under the sign-in form registers a
new school.

![The school portal sign-in, with Google and email options](/images/school/getting-started/signin.png)
*Sign in with Google, or with your school email and password.*

## The dashboard

Signing in lands on the dashboard, your school at a glance. Five figures
sit at the top:

- **Coin Balance** — your school's Akoro coin balance; tap the card to
  top up. Coins power the AI features across the portal.
- **Students** — learners enrolled across the school.
- **Instructors** — teaching staff.
- **Courses** — published and draft courses.
- **Classrooms** — active class groups.

![The dashboard figures: coin balance, students, instructors, courses, and classrooms](/images/school/getting-started/dashboard-stats.png)
*The five headline figures, updated from your school's live data.*

Under the figures, **Quick Actions** jump straight into common jobs:
schedule a **New Live Class**, **View Students**, **Add Student**, launch
a **Create Battle** quiz, build a **New On-Demand** video course, or open
the **AI Assistant**.

![Quick Actions on the dashboard](/images/school/getting-started/dashboard-actions.png)
*Six shortcuts to the jobs schools do most.*

The rest of the dashboard is live working space:

- **Your profile card** repeats your student total and active courses.
- The **AI Agent** card shows your school's teaching assistant — its
  name, whether it is active, and a **Train Agent** button for shaping
  how it answers your students.
- **Today's Schedule** lists today's live sessions across your school's
  courses, with a shortcut to schedule one when the day is empty.
- **Streaming Platforms** connects Zoom and Google Meet for live
  classes — **Connect** your account, or **Use link** to paste meeting
  links instead.
- **Social Platforms** links the school's public pages.

## Finding your way around

The left menu groups everything the school runs: Staff & Team,
Management (fees, accounts, students, academics, exams, HR, and more),
the learning Modules, Facilities & Services, and System settings. The
menu only shows modules your school owns and has switched on — see
[Module settings](/docs/school/settings/general) to change them.

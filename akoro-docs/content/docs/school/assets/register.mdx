The Assets section tracks the school's fixed assets — what it owns,
where each item is, and what state it is in. This page covers the
register itself; assignments, maintenance, audits, disposals, and
depreciation are under [Asset lifecycle](/docs/school/assets/lifecycle).

## Asset Dashboard

The **Asset Dashboard** puts fixed assets at a glance: total assets,
their combined purchase value, and how many are in use, under
maintenance, or disposed, plus any open audits.

![Asset Dashboard: totals, purchase value, and asset states](/images/school/assets/dashboard.png)
*The whole estate in five numbers.*

## Asset Register

The **Asset Register** is every asset the school owns. Filter by
category and by status — In Use, In Store, Maintenance, or Disposed —
and add an item with **Register Asset**.

## Asset Categories

**Asset Categories** group assets by type, such as Electronics.
**New Category** adds one; categories drive the register's filter, so
set them up before the register grows.

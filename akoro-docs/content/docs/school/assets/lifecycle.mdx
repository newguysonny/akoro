Once an asset is on the [register](/docs/school/assets/register), its
life is tracked here — who holds it, what it costs to keep, and when
it leaves.

## Asset Assignments

**Asset Assignments** records who holds which asset. **New
Assignment** hands an asset to a holder, so there is always a name
against an item that is out of store.

## Asset Maintenance

**Asset Maintenance** is the repairs and servicing log. **Log
Maintenance** records work done on an asset; assets under maintenance
show as such on the dashboard.

## Asset Audits

**Asset Audits** runs physical verification exercises — checking that
what the register says exists is actually there. **New Audit** starts
one, and open audits count on the dashboard until they close.

## Asset Disposals

**Asset Disposals** records assets leaving the register. **Record
Disposal** takes an item off the books, with the register keeping the
disposed item's history.

## Asset Depreciation

**Asset Depreciation** works out straight-line book values from the
register: for each asset it shows cost, age, the depreciation rate,
the accumulated depreciation, and the current book value.

![Asset Depreciation: cost, rate, accumulated depreciation, and book value](/images/school/assets/depreciation.png)
*What each asset is still worth on the books.*

## Asset Reports

**Asset Reports** summarises the register: total purchase value,
assets on the register, and the counts in use, under maintenance,
disposed, and in open audits.

## School Settings

**School Settings** is the home for how your school is set up:
**School Profile**, **System & Formats**, **Attendance**, **Privacy &
Security**, **Integrations**, **Help & Support**, and **About**. The
same page links out to external channels — Billing & Plan, Social
Media, Telegram Bot, Domain, and Notifications — and a Quick Guide.

The Help & Support tab lives here too, alongside the school's own
[help articles](/docs/school/ai-help).

![School Settings: profile, formats, attendance, integrations](/images/school/settings/home.png)
*Manage your school profile, formats and integrations from one page.*

Two details from the profile are worth knowing: the school acronym is
used in automated SMS templates to save characters, and images should
be transparent PNGs — the favicon is auto-resized, and the principal's
signature is stamped onto report cards.

## Module Management

**Module Management** enables or disables school-management features.
Disable the modules your school doesn't use to keep the side menu
clean. Core modules — Staff & Teams, Student Information, Academic
Management, and the modules covering courses, classes, students, and
assessments — are essential system components and cannot be disabled.

## Roles & Permissions

**Roles & Permissions** controls what each staff role can see and do.
**New Role** adds one; each existing role shows how many members it
has and how much of the portal it reaches, and **Edit** changes that
reach. An Administrator has full access to every module, a Teacher
reaches the classroom, homework, and gradebook areas, an Accountant
the fees and finance modules, and Front Desk the front office and
student information.

## Audit Trail

The **Audit Trail** logs every change made to your school's settings.
The activity log can be filtered by module — Backups, Roles,
Settings, Teacher Requests — and by action, and each event records
what was created, updated, or deleted, by whom, and when.

## Backup Management

**Backup Management** snapshots and restores your school's data.
**Create Backup** takes a manual snapshot; the page totals your
backups and the storage they use, and lists the backup history —
nightly automatic backups alongside manual ones. Under the auto
backup schedule you can enable auto backup, choose a frequency
(Daily, Weekly, or Monthly), set how many days backups are retained,
and **Save Schedule**. Backups older than the retention window are
pruned automatically; manual backups are never auto-deleted.

## Manage Subscription

**Manage Subscription** shows your current plan: what it covers, its
student limit, its status, and when the subscription ends. You can
renew or upgrade at any time — select a plan on the page and proceed
to payment, paying monthly or yearly.

![Manage Subscription: your current plan](/images/school/settings/subscription.png)
*View your plan and upgrade at any time.*

## Payment History

**Payment History** keeps your subscription payment records: order,
plan, amount, payment method, transaction ID, proof, status, and
date. A payment that is still pending carries a **Pay Now** action so
it can be completed from the list.

## Payment Gateway Settings

**Payment Gateway Settings** lets parents pay school fees online
through the Parent Portal. Choose a payment provider, then set the
payment mode — Sandbox for test payments, or Live to accept real
payments — and enter the live and sandbox credentials for that
provider. The webhook secret is required: it is how payments are
securely confirmed. An optional online payment surcharge can be added
on top of what parents pay. These are the checkouts behind the
[online payments](/docs/school/fees/transactions) in Fees.

## Tool & Asset Purchases

**Tool & Asset Purchases** lists your Tools & Assets marketplace
payments: each management tool or asset the school has taken, whether
it was free or paid, and when. Purchases made in the
[Apps Center](/docs/school/settings/admissions-and-apps) are recorded
here.

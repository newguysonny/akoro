## Admission Form Configuration

**Admission Form Configuration** sets up your public admission form
and its fee. Under Fee & Payment Settings you can **Enable Admission
Fee**, set the fee amount, and upload a payment QR code image (png or
jpg, up to 2MB). The custom public URL slug becomes your admission
link — the address families use to apply.

Under Custom Form Fields, **Add New Field** extends the form: text
input, number, date, dropdown, file, textarea, or checkbox, each
optionally a required field. The field list shows each field's order,
label, type, and whether it is required. The page also carries your
direct admission link, to **Copy** and share, and **Preview
Admission** to see the form as families see it. Applications sent
through it arrive under
[Online Admissions](/docs/school/students/admissions).

**Save Settings** applies the configuration.

## Apps Center

The **Apps Center** is where you browse and unlock tools & assets for
your agent. Unlocking platform tools and exam resources powers up
what the AI agent can generate — lesson plans, worksheets, reports,
and more. The catalogue splits into Management Tools, Agent Tools,
and Assets, and the page counts what you have unlocked in each.
Payments for unlocks are covered under
[Billing](/docs/school/settings/billing).

## Profile

**Profile** manages your own info and your AI agent. The Profile tab
holds your personal info — full name, username, email, bio, phone,
and country — your teaching details (school or centre, institution
type, subjects taught), and the tool templates your agent uses by
default for lesson plans, worksheets, and reports. The Agent tab
holds the agent's name and personality. The school agent itself is
covered under
[Courses & battles](/docs/school/learning/courses-and-battles).

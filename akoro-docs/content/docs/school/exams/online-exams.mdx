## Manage Online Exams

**Manage Online Exams** runs computer-based tests: each online exam
has a status — Draft, Published, or Closed — and **New Online Exam**
creates one. Publish when the test is ready for students to sit;
close it when the window ends.

## Question Bank

The **Question Bank** holds reusable multiple-choice questions,
filterable by subject. **New Question** adds one. Build the bank once
and online exams draw from it, instead of retyping questions for every
test.

![Question Bank: filter by subject, add questions once](/images/school/exams/question-bank.png)
*Reusable questions, organised by subject.*

Online exams are separate from the offline examination events — a
school can run paper exams, computer-based tests, or both in the same
term.

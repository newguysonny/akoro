title: Marks & grades

---

## Enter Marks

**Enter Marks** records scores per exam paper: select the paper (set
up under [Schedule & Marks Setup](/docs/school/exams/exam-setup)) and
enter each student's score. Marks entered here flow into marksheets
and report cards.

## Manage Grades

**Manage Grades** is the academic grading scale — the bands scores
fall into and the grade each band earns. **New Grade** adds a band.
Report cards and marksheets grade with this scale, so set it before
results are generated.

## Cocurricular areas and grades

**Cocurricular Areas** lists the activities beyond the classroom —
Sports, Music, Debate Club, and your own under **New Area**.
**Cocurricular Grades** is their grading scale, maintained the same
way, so report cards can grade activities as well as subjects.

The Front Office section is the school's reception desk: who is
asking about admission, who came through the gate, what has been
complained about, and what arrived in the post. The **Front Office
Dashboard** counts it all at a glance — admission enquiries, new
enquiries, visitors today, open complaints, and postal records.

![Front Office Dashboard: reception activity at a glance](/images/school/front-office/dashboard.png)
*Enquiries, visitors, complaints, and post — the day's front desk.*

## Admission Enquiries

**Admission Enquiries** tracks prospective parent enquiries from
first contact to a decision. Each enquiry carries a status — New,
Contacted, Admitted, or Closed — and **New Enquiry** logs one.
Enquiries that end in admission feed the same intake as
[Admissions](/docs/school/students/admissions).

## Visitor Book

The **Visitor Book** records who came in and why: visitor name,
phone, purpose, who they came to meet, and their check-in and
check-out times — anyone still inside shows as On Premises. **Log
Visitor** signs someone in at the desk.

![Visitor Book: who came in, why, and whether they have left](/images/school/front-office/visitor-book.png)
*Every visitor, signed in and out.*

## Complaints

**Complaints** holds issues raised by parents and visitors, each with
a complainant, a category, and a description. Work them through the
statuses — Open, In Progress, Resolved — and log new ones with **New
Complaint**. Open complaints count on the dashboard until they are
resolved.

## Postal Records

**Postal Records** logs incoming and outgoing mail: direction
(Received or Dispatched), a reference number, the item, who it came
from and who it is for, and the date. **New Record** adds an entry, so
documents handed to the front desk can always be traced.

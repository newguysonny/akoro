## Student Admission

**Student Admission** registers a new student in five steps:

1. **Academic** — admission number and roll number (both can be
   generated automatically), admission date, class and section, and a
   biometric ID if your school uses one. A student arriving from another
   school can carry an **Opening Due Balance**, which then shows in
   their fee ledger.
2. **Personal Info** — the student's own details.
3. **Parents** — parent and guardian contacts.
4. **Health & Bank** — health notes and payment details.
5. **Documents** — the student's papers.

## Online Admissions

Families can also apply through your public website's **Apply page**
(see the [website builder](/docs/school/website-builder/build)). **Online
Admissions** lists those applications — applicant, class applied for,
fee, and submission date — with status filters: All, Pending, Reviewed,
Admitted, Rejected. Work the queue to review each application and admit
or reject it; admitted applicants become students on the register. The
form itself is configured under
[Admission settings](/docs/school/settings/admissions-and-apps).

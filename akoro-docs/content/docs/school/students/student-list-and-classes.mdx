## Student List

**Student List** is the register: every student with admission number,
roll number, class, section, fees assigned and due, and status. Filter
by class, section, and status (Active, Inactive, Alumni), and page at
10, 25, 50, or 100 entries.

The actions above the list cover bulk work:

- **Admit Student** — the admission form.
- **Enrol / Invite** — bring an existing Akoro learner into the school.
- **Bulk Upload** — import students in bulk.
- **Bulk Photos** and **Bulk Docs** — attach photos and documents for
  many students at once.
- Export the list with Copy, CSV, Excel, PDF, or Print, control column
  visibility, and use **Bulk Edit** / **Bulk Delete** on selections.

## Assign to Class

**Assign to Class** bulk-places students into a class — including
students who have none yet. Pick the source list (unassigned students,
all students, or a class), tick the students, choose the target class
and an optional section, and **Assign**.

![Assign to Class: source, target class, and optional section](/images/school/students/assign-class.png)
*Bulk-place students, starting with those who have no class.*

## Houses and categories

**Student Houses** are the sports and pastoral houses students belong
to; each house has a name and a colour, and **New House** adds one.
**Student Categories** group students another way — the common pair is
Day Student and Boarding Student — and **New Category** adds your own.
Both feed filters and reports across the portal.

## Disabled Students

**Disabled Students** lists students flagged with a disability, by
class, so support can be planned and counted (the count also appears on
the Student Dashboard).

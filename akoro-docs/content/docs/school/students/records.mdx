## Behavior Records

**Behavior Records** logs student behaviour incidents. **New Incident**
records one against a student; the list keeps the school's incident
history in one place, and the count appears on the Student Dashboard.

## Health Records

**Health Records** holds allergies, conditions, vaccinations, and
checkups. **New Record** adds an entry for a student, so medical
information is on hand when it is needed — at the clinic, on the field,
or on a trip.

## Learner Struggles

**Learner Struggles** is where the ERP meets the learning app: for
learners with a linked Akoro account, it shows where they are stuck in
their studies — and what helped them through. Per class it counts linked
learners, how many are struggling, how many cases are open, and how
many were overcome in the last 30 days.

![Learner Struggles: linked learners, struggling, open, and overcome counts](/images/school/students/learner-struggles.png)
*See which linked learners are stuck, and whether they got through it.*

Open a learner to see the topic they are struggling with and follow up
— a conversation, extra support, or a word with their teacher — before
a small block becomes a failed term.

---
title: Students & admissions
description: Admit students, keep the register, place them in classes, houses, and categories, and keep behaviour and health records.
---

The Student Information section is the school's register. Its dashboard
counts the student body — total, active, present and absent today,
disabled students, behaviour records, houses, and categories — and the
pages under it run the student lifecycle:

- [Admissions](/docs/school/students/admissions) — the five-step
  admission form, and applications from your public Apply page.
- [Student list & classes](/docs/school/students/student-list-and-classes) —
  the full register with bulk tools, class placement, houses, and
  categories.
- [Records](/docs/school/students/records) — behaviour, health, and
  where linked learners are struggling.
- [Promote students](/docs/school/students/promote-students) — move a
  class up at the end of session.

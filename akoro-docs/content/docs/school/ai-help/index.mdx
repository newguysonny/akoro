Help in the portal works two ways: your school keeps its own library
of help articles for staff, and anyone who is stuck can ask the school
agent directly.

## Help categories

**Manage Categories** organises help articles by topic. **New
Category** adds a topic, so articles can be filed under the area they
belong to — fees, exams, attendance, and the rest of the modules you
use.

## Browse articles

**Browse Articles** is the library itself: how-to guides and FAQs,
filterable by category. **New Article** adds a guide, so the answers
your school works out once stay written down for the next person who
needs them.

## AI Chatbot

**AI Chatbot** is where staff ask the school agent questions. The
agent already knows your school, so you can ask it how to use any
module, draft notices, summarise records, or troubleshoot — then
continue the conversation in AI Chat with **Open AI Chat**.

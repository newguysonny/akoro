# Instructor app

The Instructor app is the teacher-facing surface of Akoro. Instructors create
courses, run live and on-demand lessons, manage students, set assignments and
assessments, and track earnings.

## Courses and the builder

Instructors create and manage courses with a course builder, then publish
lessons, media and materials. Course video uploads encode asynchronously — see
[Course media](/docs/build/media).

## Students and work

- Enrol and manage students.
- Create assignments and submissions.
- Author and grade assessments.
- Track tasks and progress.

## Documents and library

Instructors keep their own library of documents and materials to reuse across
courses.

## Wallet and payouts

Earnings, fees and payouts are handled through the wallet.

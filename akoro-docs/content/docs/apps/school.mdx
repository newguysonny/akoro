# School app

The School app is the institution-facing surface of Akoro. It is a full school
management system, not a small admin panel, with more than 170 API route
declarations and 128 management tables.

## Modules

- **Admissions** — applications, enrolment and onboarding.
- **Students** — records, classes and guardians.
- **Academics** — curriculum, timetable and syllabus.
- **Exams** — scheduling, grading and results.
- **Fees** — billing, payments and reconciliation.
- **HR and payroll** — staff records and payroll.
- **Library and inventory** — school resources.
- **Transport and hostel** — logistics and boarding.
- **Assets** — school property and equipment.
- **Website builder** — school site and domain management.
- **Settings** — school configuration.

## Public API

Most school ERP routes are internal and are not part of the public API. The
public surface is an allow-listed subset; see [API reference](/docs/reference/api).

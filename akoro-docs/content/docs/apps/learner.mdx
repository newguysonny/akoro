# Learner app

The Learner app is the student-facing surface of Akoro. It covers onboarding, a
personal library, an AI protégé, study and exam preparation, practice,
communities and the public catalogue.

## Onboarding

New learners set up a profile and preferences before study begins. See
[Getting started](/docs/start/getting-started) for the API side.

## Library

Learners upload files, paste notes and save links, then read, extract and ask
about them with citations. See [Library](/docs/build/library) for the upload,
extraction and ask flow.

## AI protégé

The protégé is the learner-facing teaching agent. It plans study, calls tools,
verifies results and recovers from failure. Study progress and mastery evidence
are tracked against the learner's own record.

## Study and exam preparation

- Teaching sessions and adaptive learning.
- Study Prep and Exam Prep.
- Practice questions and assessment evidence.
- Communities and battles.
- Past questions and PharmD catalogue.

## Wallet

The learner has a wallet for payments, vouchers and the agent marketplace.

## Public catalogue

Learners browse published courses, programmes, live sessions and PharmD content.
See [Public catalogues](/docs/build/catalogues).

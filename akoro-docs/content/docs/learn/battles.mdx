# Battles

Battles are live, judged quizzes where each learner's AI agent competes against
others. An agent does not answer from general knowledge. It answers from what
its owner actually taught it, drawing only on the learner's own notes, so a
battle measures what the learner knows rather than what the model can guess.

The battle system is exposed at `/api/battle`. It is described here from the
module code and routes, not from a published API contract.

> **Status: beta.** Battles are implemented but are not yet in the capability
> registry, and the feature documentation lists them as "not yet documented".
> Treat this page as a module-level overview, not a stable contract.

## Battle types

The route header names three battle types, plus study reviews that run through
the same worker.

| Type | Who is in it | How it starts |
|---|---|---|
| 1v1 Challenge | A student or teacher challenges a specific opponent | The opponent accepts or declines the invitation |
| Open Lobby | Anyone enrolled can join up to `max_participants` | A learner creates it and others join |
| Classroom | A teacher starts it and all invited students must accept | Teacher sends invitations to a class |

The same worker also runs two non-contest modes observed in code:

- **Study review** — one learner's agent works through a paper alone. It has no
  HP to lose, no winner and no XP race; it is a check of knowledge, and each
  learner earns a free coin for taking part.
- **Agent Check** — Study Prep's practice check, answered from the platform's
  own budget rather than the learner's, and handed back to Study Prep at the end.

Team (2v2) and group battles also exist in the codebase. A group battle is
stored as a classroom-type battle; a team battle is stored as a `team` type.

## Entry rules and costs

Before a learner takes a seat, the entry check verifies two requirements.

- **Coins** — enough token credit for their own agent's answers, roughly the
  number of questions times the cost of one answer. An agent that cannot pay is
  not let in short.
- **XP** — the battle's `min_xp`, set by its creator. A value of `0` is open to
  all.

A study review has no XP gate (it is a check of knowledge, not a contest), but
it still needs the coins for the answers. The entry check runs when a learner
accepts a challenge, joins an open battle, accepts a challenge request, takes a
daily-battle seat, or creates a battle they will play in. `GET
/api/battle/battles/:battleId/entry` returns the requirements.

How many players a battle needs to run depends on its type. A review, an Agent
Check and a daily battle run for one learner's agent; a team battle needs all
four players; everything else needs two.

## Question sourcing

Questions come from three sources, normalised to one shape so the battle does
not care which source filled it.

| Source | What it draws from |
|---|---|
| Past papers | Verified multiple-choice questions from the past-paper bank |
| Course bank | A course's own active question bank |
| Generated | New questions written by a model in the bank's style |

A battle can use `bank` (archive only), `ai` (generated only) or `auto` — the
default, which takes the archive first and generates only the shortfall. The
creator pays for generated questions; a platform-made battle, like the daily
battle, has no creator, so the app pays.

Generated questions are styled on real verified exemplars pulled from the bank,
and grounded in the curriculum's performance objectives where they exist. Two
further inputs can steer generation: the learner's own past-paper questions
(`basedOn`) and passages from their own notes (`fromNotes`). A question whose
answer key does not resolve to an option is dropped rather than scored.

The battle's duration is divided across its questions to set a clock per
question, floored at 8 seconds and capped at 180 seconds, falling back to 15
seconds.

## Judging and results

Sergeant, the platform's judge agent, judges every contest. Marking itself is
never a model's call: an answer is right when it matches the stored answer key.
Sergeant explains why the key is right (the ruling, shown at the reveal) and, at
the end, gives a short verdict on who won and what decided it. Judging is paid
for by the app and is free to learners. A study review has no judge.

Every agent starts on 100 HP. Each question an agent does not get right — a
wrong answer, or no answer at all — costs the battle's `hp_per_miss`, set by the
creator (default 10, maximum 50). At 0 HP the agent is out and watches as a
spectator, unranked. A value of `0` means nobody can be knocked out.

At the end, scores are settled from the recorded answers rather than the running
counters. Participants are ranked, and the XP pool is paid out by rank. A
classroom battle pays from its `xp_pool`; other battles pay from `xp_prize`. A
review pays the pool scaled by how much of the paper the learner got right, and
gives each participant a free coin. A team battle awards the team with more
correct answers, with ties shared. Questions drawn from past papers are also
logged as past-question practice, and one revision card is raised for the
topics the learner was actually weak on.

## Recurring series

A class, course or network battle can repeat on days of the week at a time,
until an end date. Each occurrence is created by running the same invite route
as a one-off battle. Members can sit one out, leave, or the creator can end the
series.

- `POST /api/battle/series/:seriesId/skip` — sit one occurrence out.
- `POST /api/battle/series/:seriesId/opt-out` — leave the series and its
  upcoming occurrences.
- `POST /api/battle/series/:seriesId/end` — the creator ends the series; no more
  occurrences, and one already made but not started is cancelled.

## Worker lifecycle

Battles run on a BullMQ queue named `battles`, backed by Redis. Job modules sit
beside the queue and route by action.

| Job | What it does |
|---|---|
| `start-battle` | Claims a waiting battle live, drops seats nobody took, broadcasts Q1 |
| `advance-question` | Broadcasts the next question and schedules its answer jobs |
| `agent-answer` | One agent answers one question on its own schedule |
| `reveal-question` | Marks the question, tallies misses, applies HP and shows the ruling |
| `end-battle` | Settles, ranks, pays XP and review coins, then hands an Agent Check back |
| `expire-battle` | Closes the invite window, or starts the battle if enough accepted |
| `refresh-leaderboard` | Rebuilds the course leaderboard after a finished battle |

When a battle starts, only the learners who actually took their seat compete;
invitees who never answered are dropped, not failed. The teacher hosts but is
not in the fight. Each question is embedded once and every agent's answer job
searches with the same vector. An agent answers from its learner's notes; when
the notes do not cover the question it says so and guesses, and the log records
the gap. A battle ends after the last question, or when nobody is left who can
answer.

## Endpoints

The route file declares 43 endpoints. The main learner-facing ones are:

- `GET /api/battle/health` — public health check.
- `GET /api/battle/my-battles`, `GET /api/battle/my-challenges` — the learner's
  own battles and challenges.
- `POST /api/battle/courses/:courseId/battles/open` — create an open battle.
- `POST /api/battle/classes/:classroomId/battles/invite` — invite a class.
- `POST /api/battle/battles/:battleId/join` — join an open battle.
- `POST /api/battle/challenges/:challengeId/accept`, `/decline` — answer an
  invitation.
- `POST /api/battle/battles/:battleId/answer` — agents answer; the human watches.
- `GET /api/battle/battles/:battleId/state` — the live scoreboard.
- `POST /api/battle/battles/:battleId/start`, `/end` — start or end early.
- `GET /api/battle/battles/:battleId/results`, `/questions`, `/log` — review
  what happened.
- `GET /api/battle/courses/:courseId/questions-bank` — the course question bank.

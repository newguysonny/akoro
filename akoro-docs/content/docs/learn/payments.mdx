# Payments, wallet and vouchers

Akoro handles money in a few connected surfaces: a learner coin wallet that pays
for AI features, instructor earnings that are settled as bank payouts, a voucher
marketplace for credit codes, course and cart purchases, and learner
subscriptions. This page describes the product concepts. The provider webhook
that credits and settles all of this is documented separately in
[Webhooks and callbacks](/docs/build/webhooks).

## Wallet

Each learner has a coin wallet. Coins are the unit that AI features — tutoring,
study planning, quiz and assessment generation, and voice — are priced in, so a
wallet balance is how a learner pays for them.

- **Balance and transactions** — the wallet shows the current coin balance and a
  recent transaction history. Each transaction records the coin change, the
  balance after it, the feature it paid for, and an identifier for the charge.
- **Welcome grant** — a newly created wallet starts with a welcome grant of
  coins so a learner can try the AI features before topping up.
- **Low balance** — the wallet reports a low-balance threshold so the learner
  app can prompt a top-up before a charge is blocked.
- **School wallets** — a school has its own wallet. A school admin can top up
  the school wallet and allocate coins to individual students. When a learner's
  own balance runs out, the charge can fall back to their school's wallet.

### Funding a wallet

A wallet is funded in two ways, both of which credit the same coin balance:

1. **Top-up in naira** — the learner (or a school admin, for the school wallet)
   starts a top-up and pays through the payment provider. The wallet is credited
   once the provider confirms the payment.
2. **Voucher code** — the learner redeems a voucher code, which credits coins to
   their wallet. One voucher credit equals one coin.

A pricing endpoint exposes the current coin value, the platform markup and a few
example costs (such as a tutor message, a quiz generation and an assessment) so
the apps can show what a coin buys.

## Instructor earnings and payouts

Every course sale creates an immutable earnings record for the course's
instructor. Each record snapshots the amounts at the time of purchase, so a
later change to the platform commission never alters settled earnings.

- **Gross, commission, net** — each sale records the gross amount, the platform
  commission and the instructor's net share. The commission percentage is
  applied at purchase time and stored with the record.
- **Earnings overview** — instructors see their earnings as a summary plus
  per-course and per-month breakdowns, and the admin sees a platform-wide view.
- **Bank details** — before requesting a payout, the instructor verifies a bank
  account number and saves the account details.
- **Payouts** — an instructor requests a payout of all pending earnings. A
  payout is subject to a minimum threshold and is sent to the saved bank
  account. Payouts move through processing to completed, and a failed or
  reversed transfer returns the earnings to the pending balance so the
  instructor can retry.

## Vouchers and credits

Vouchers are a self-contained credit marketplace with two distinct sides.

- **Learner side** — a learner redeems a voucher code to credit coins to their
  wallet, or pays for a cart with a voucher. Redemption happens server-side and
  a single-use code cannot be credited twice.
- **Business side** — a business (a "startup") registers, sets a per-credit rate
  and discount tiers, and sells batches of voucher codes to agents. Agents top
  up a prepaid wallet, buy codes, and track their codes and purchase history.
- **Redemption API** — a startup's own backend validates codes through a
  server-to-server API authenticated with an API key. A successful redemption
  tells the startup how many credits a code carries and credits the startup net
  of a platform fee. Startups withdraw their settlement balance to a bank
  account.

## Purchases and refunds

- **Courses and carts** — a learner buys a single course or checks out a whole
  cart in one payment, including courses and teaching guides. Free items are
  granted directly without a payment.
- **Fulfilment** — once the provider confirms payment, the learner is enrolled,
  the instructor's earnings are recorded, and the buyer is notified. A cart
  payment enrols every item in the cart, not just the first.
- **Refunds** — a learner can request a refund within the course's refund window
  (30 days by default), and an admin can refund any successful payment. A refund
  marks the payment and the instructor's earnings as refunded and removes the
  learner's access to every item the payment covered.

## Subscriptions

The learner Premium tier is sold as a monthly or annual subscription. A learner
picks a cycle and pays through the provider; a paid invoice, subscription
timeline entry and audit record are created on success. Renewals charge the
saved card automatically. A failed renewal moves the subscription past due and
into a retry cycle before it eventually returns to the free tier.

## Notifications

Payments raise in-app notifications through a stable event catalogue. The
payment-related events are:

| Event | Trigger | Recipient |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `purchase_success` | A payment is confirmed and access granted | Buyer |
| `order_processing` | A payment is being confirmed | Buyer |
| `purchase_refunded` | A refund is processed and access removed | Buyer |
| `sale_new` | A new course sale is recorded | Instructor |
| `payout_completed` | A payout is sent to the bank account | Instructor |
| `payout_failed` | A payout could not be completed and funds were returned | Instructor |

These are the same event types surfaced over the notification socket — see
[Realtime and sockets](/docs/build/realtime).

## Webhook verification

Payments, top-ups, payouts and refunds are all settled from provider-signed
webhooks. Signature verification and reliability are covered in
[Webhooks and callbacks](/docs/build/webhooks) and are not repeated here.

# Assessments

**Assessment Review** is where proctored exams are policed and their
results reviewed: proctored exam integrity and results, in one queue.

![Assessment Review](/images/instructor/assessments/assessments.png)
_Proctored exam integrity and results._

## The three views

- **Review queue** — proctored sessions waiting for your judgement.
- **Flagged** — sessions the proctoring marked for integrity concerns.
- **Terminated** — sessions that were ended early.

When a view has nothing in it, it says **No assessments in this view** —
an empty queue is good news, not an error.

## Working the queue

Open a session from the queue to see what the proctoring recorded, then
decide: a flagged session you clear stands; a session you uphold follows
your school's rules for the learner. Terminated sessions stay on record
so the decision trail is never lost.

# Activities

Activities is the register of everything you have set for learners —
every type, every stage, in one list.

![The Activities page](/images/instructor/activities/activities.png)
_Everything you've set for learners — every type, every stage._

## The counters

At the top: **Total**, **Live Now**, **Scheduled** and **Completed** —
how much work is out with learners right now, what is waiting for its
moment, and what is done.

## Filter the list

- **Stage** — All, Live, Scheduled, Lobby or Completed.
- **Type** — Self study, Assignment, Project, Live course, Battle or
  Multi stage.
- **Date** — Any date, Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, or a Custom
  range.

## Creating an activity

Activities are prepared with your agent. When you have none yet, the
page says so: **No activities yet** — "Ask your agent to prepare one —
you review the draft in chat, then assign it." The quick starts take
you straight there:

- **Prepare an assignment**
- **Set a battle**
- **Schedule a live course**
- **Start a self study**

**Create in chat** at the top opens the same path in
[your agent's chat](/docs/instructor/agent).

## Question bank

![The Question bank](/images/instructor/activities/activities-question-bank.png)
_Questions you can reuse across activities._

The **Question bank** holds your reusable questions, so a good question
is written once and set many times — in assignments, quizzes and exam
prep alike.

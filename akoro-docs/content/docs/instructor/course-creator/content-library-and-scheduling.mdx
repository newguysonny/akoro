# Content library and scheduling

## Content Library

The **Content Library** is the Course Creator's store of materials —
the content items your courses are assembled from. The creator home
counts them as **Content items** across all your courses. Build a
piece of content once, and it can serve every course that needs it,
instead of being rebuilt course by course.

## Schedule sessions

![Schedule Session](/images/instructor/course-creator/creator-schedule-sessions.png)
_Put a live class on the calendar._

**Schedule sessions** opens the **Schedule Session** form — "Put a
live class on the calendar":

- **Course** — the course the session belongs to.
- **Session Title** — for example, "Live Q&A Session".
- **Date** and **Time**.
- **Platform** — Zoom by default; the streaming platforms you connect
  on [Home](/docs/instructor/home).
- **Duration (min)** — 60 to start.
- **Meeting Link (optional)** — paste it if you already have one.

**Schedule Session** books it: the session appears in your learners'
schedules and in **Today's Schedule** on your dashboard.

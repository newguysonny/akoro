# My courses

**My Courses** lists every course you are building, with the facts that
identify it at a glance.

![My courses, with the status filter](/images/instructor/course-creator/creator-my-courses.png)
_Courses you are building._

For each course you see:

- its **subject** and title — for example *Mathematics · JSS Mathematics*;
- its **item count** — how many content items it holds;
- its **type and status** — for example *Video course · Published*.

## Filter by status

The filter row starts at **Any status** and narrows the list to:

- **Draft** — still being built; only you can see it.
- **In review** — submitted and waiting on review.
- **Published** — live for learners to enroll.

**+ New course** at the top starts another course without leaving the
list.

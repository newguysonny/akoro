# Course Creator

The Course Creator is where your courses live. Its home answers "how are
your courses doing?" and its sections take you to your course list, your
content library and your session schedule.

![The Course Creator home](/images/instructor/course-creator/course-creator.png)
_The creator's own sidebar appears under Course Creator in the main navigation._

## The creator home

Four figures summarise your catalogue:

- **Courses** — every course you are building.
- **Content items** — the lessons and materials inside them.
- **Published** — courses live for learners.
- **In review** — courses waiting on review before they can publish.

**+ New course** starts a new one; **Content library** jumps to your
reusable content. **Needs your attention** lists anything waiting on
you — when there is nothing, it says **All caught up.**

## Start a new course

**+ New course** opens the builder. "Start with the basics. You'll add
sections and lessons next."

![New course](/images/instructor/course-creator/creator-new-course.png)
_Start with the basics — sections and lessons come next._

- **Course type** — for example, a video course of recorded lessons.
- **Course title** — what learners will see.
- **Category** — the subject area.
- **Level** — Beginner and up.
- **Language**.
- **Tags** — optional, separated with commas.

**Create course** makes it; you then build it out section by section
and lesson by lesson, and it joins [My courses](/docs/instructor/course-creator/my-courses)
as a draft until you publish.

## The sections

- [My courses](/docs/instructor/course-creator/my-courses) — every
  course, filterable by **Draft**, **In review** and **Published**.
- [Content library and scheduling](/docs/instructor/course-creator/content-library-and-scheduling)
  — the materials you reuse across courses, and the live sessions you
  schedule for them.

## How a course is built

A course is built from content items — lessons, videos and materials —
grouped inside the course. Drafts stay private until you submit them;
a course in review cannot be edited into a different course underneath
the review. Once published, learners can enroll, and the course starts
counting in your [Home](/docs/instructor/home) figures and earning in
[Sales & Earnings](/docs/instructor/money/sales-and-earnings).

# Students

**Student Information** is your class list and everything attached to
it. It has four sections: **My Students**, **Join Requests**,
**Attendance** and **Behavior Records**.

## My Students

![My Students](/images/instructor/students/students.png)
_Your learners, their classes and their progress._

The table lists every learner enrolled with you:

- **Student** and **Email** — who they are and how they sign in.
- **Class** — their school class (or **No class**), and how many of
  your courses they are in.
- **Progress** — how far through the course they are, as a bar and a
  percentage.
- **Status** — for example **Active**.

**Search by name or email…** finds a learner fast. The list pages —
twelve at a time — and **Export CSV** downloads it. **Add Student**
enrolls a new learner; the checkboxes and row actions let you work
with learners in bulk or one at a time.

## Join Requests

![Join Requests](/images/instructor/students/students-join-requests.png)
_Learners who asked to join your class — approve to add them to your roster._

**Join Requests** holds learners who asked to join your class. Approve
one to add them to your roster — they appear in My Students once they
are in. When nobody is waiting, the page says **No pending join
requests**: "When a learner asks to join your class, they'll appear
here for approval."

## Attendance

![Attendance Register](/images/instructor/students/students-attendance.png)
_Mark a whole class in one screen._

The **Attendance Register** marks a whole class in one screen. Pick a
**class**, a section (or **All sections**) and a **date** — "Pick a
class and date to take attendance" — and mark the roll. Your agent can
also take roll call from its templates in
[chat](/docs/instructor/agent); the register itself lives here.

## Behavior Records

![Behavior Records](/images/instructor/students/students-behavior-records.png)
_Log positive and negative behavior._

**Behavior Records** logs positive and negative behavior for your
learners. **Search**, or filter by **Student** and **Type**, and use
**New Record** to log an entry — the record that sits beside a
learner's progress when you, the school or a parent ask how they are
doing.

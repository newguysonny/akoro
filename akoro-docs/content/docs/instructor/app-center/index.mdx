# App Center

The App Center is where your agent's abilities come from: platform
tools for your agent, and exam resources to unlock.

![The App Center](/images/instructor/app-center/app-center.png)
_Platform tools for your agent, and exam resources to unlock._

## Platform tools

**Power up your teaching**: every platform tool is included — your
agent uses them to generate lesson plans, worksheets, reports and
more. There is nothing to buy per tool; the counters at the top count
your **Tools**, **Assets** and how much you have **Unlocked**. The
**Tools** and **Assets** tabs split the catalogue when it fills.

When the catalogue has nothing for you yet, it says **Nothing here
yet — check back soon.**

## Exam resources

Unlock exam resources to add **past papers and mark schemes** to what
your agent can draw on. Unlocked resources appear in your
[Library](/docs/instructor/library) under Exam resources, ready for
exam prep.

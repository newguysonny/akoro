# Submissions

Submissions is where learner work comes back to you. Its job is simple:
grade submitted work, activity by activity.

![The Submissions page](/images/instructor/submissions/submissions.png)
_Grade submitted work, activity by activity._

## How it is organised

- **Submitted activities** — the activities that have work waiting.
  Pick an activity to see its submissions.
- **Who to grade first** — a count of what is waiting, ordered so the
  most pressing grading comes first.

**New activity** at the top takes you to setting more work — activities
are prepared with your agent and reviewed in chat before they are
assigned, as described in [Activities](/docs/instructor/activities).

## Grading

Open an activity, work through its submissions, and grade each one.
Grades you give flow into the [Gradebook](/docs/instructor/academic/gradebook),
where they join the weights and the grading scale to produce final
grades.

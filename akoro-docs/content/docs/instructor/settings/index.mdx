# Settings

Settings manages your preferences and account security, in sections.

![Settings](/images/instructor/settings/settings-top.png)
_Manage your preferences and account security._

## Billing

**Order history** — your tool and asset purchases from the
[App Center](/docs/instructor/app-center).

## AI Agent

**My agent tools** — the tools your AI Chat agent built for you, the
same self-built tools you manage from the agent itself.

## Personalization

- **Language** — English, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, Pidgin, French or
  Arabic.
- **Dark Mode** — currently in preview.
- **Accent Colour** — your app's accent.

## Notifications

Choose what reaches you: **New student enrolled**, **Session
reminders**, **Battle challenges**, **Task deadlines** and **Platform
updates** — plus **In-app sounds** and **Desktop push**.

## Privacy and security

- **Change Password** — update your account password.
- **Two-Factor Auth** — add an extra layer of security.
- **Active Sessions** — view and revoke active sessions.
- **Deactivate Account** — temporarily disable your account.
- **Delete Account** — permanently remove all your data.

## Help and support

**Help Centre** for guides and FAQs, **Report a Bug** when something
is not working right, and **Contact Us** to talk to the Akoro team.

## About

**Terms of Service**, **Privacy Policy** and the **App Version**.
**Sign Out** ends your session on this device.

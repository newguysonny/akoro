# Wallet and AI credits

Your wallet holds **coins** — the credit every AI feature draws from.
Top up coins here; when the balance runs out, AI work pauses until you
do.

![Wallet and AI Credits](/images/instructor/money/wallet.png)
_Top up coins — every AI feature draws from this balance._

## Your balance

The balance card shows your coins and the **Top up** packs:

| Pack | Price |
| --- | --- |
| 500 coins | ≈ ₦800 |
| 2,000 coins | ≈ ₦3,200 |
| 5,000 coins | ≈ ₦8,000 |
| 20,000 coins | ≈ ₦32,000 |

Pick a pack and **Continue to checkout** to pay.

## What things cost

The wallet shows example prices so you can plan — a tutor message is
about **2 coins**, quiz generation about **7 coins**, and an assessment
about **32 coins**. Longer and heavier jobs cost more; the balance in
the header drops as work completes.

## Voucher codes

**Top up with a voucher code** redeems a code instead of paying — enter
it and the coins land in the same balance.

## Recent activity

Every movement is listed under **Recent activity**: grants (a new
account starts with a **welcome grant** of coins), top-ups and spend,
each with its date and amount.

# Sales and earnings

**Sales & Earnings** is where your teaching turns into money. The
header tells you your sales count and the platform fee; the cards and
the table do the rest.

![Sales and Earnings](/images/instructor/money/sales.png)
_Your earnings, and every sale behind them._

## The four figures

- **Net Earned** — what you have earned after the platform fee.
- **Pending Payout** — earned, and on its way to you.
- **Paid Out** — what has already reached you.
- **Total Sales** — how many sales you have made.

## Every sale, listed

The table shows each sale: the **Learner**, the **Course**, the
**Gross** they paid, **Your Net** after the fee, its **Status** and the
**Date**. When you have no sales yet it says so — "No sales yet —
they'll appear here when learners buy your courses."

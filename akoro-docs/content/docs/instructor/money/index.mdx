# Money

Two pages look after your money:

- [Wallet & Credits](/docs/instructor/money/wallet-and-credits) — the
  coins you spend. Every AI feature draws from this balance: agent
  answers, quiz generation, assessments.
- [Sales & Earnings](/docs/instructor/money/sales-and-earnings) — the
  money you earn. Course sales, your net after the platform fee, and
  payouts.

They are separate on purpose: coins are prepaid credit for AI work,
while earnings are real money from learners buying your courses. Your
coin balance also rides in the app's header, so you always know where
you stand before you start an AI job.

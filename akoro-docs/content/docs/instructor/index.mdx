# Instructor guides

These guides cover the Akoro instructor app screen by screen: your
dashboard, the Course Creator, your AI teaching agent, your students and
classes, grading, and your wallet and earnings. Every guide shows the real
screens and names every button exactly as the app does.

## Start here

- [Getting started](/docs/instructor/getting-started) — create your
  instructor account, sign in, and install the app.

## Guides

- [Home](/docs/instructor/home) — your dashboard: balances, students,
  courses, revenue, quick actions and today's schedule.
- [Course Creator](/docs/instructor/course-creator) — build and publish
  courses, manage your content library, and schedule live sessions.
- [Your agent](/docs/instructor/agent) — chat with your AI teaching
  assistant, give it tasks, and see how it is performing.
- [Students](/docs/instructor/students) — your learners, join requests,
  attendance and behaviour records.
- [Academic](/docs/instructor/academic) — timetables, curriculum,
  syllabus, subjects, gradebook and reviewing AI lessons.
- [Activities](/docs/instructor/activities) — everything you have set for
  learners, and your question bank.
- [Submissions](/docs/instructor/submissions) — grade submitted work,
  activity by activity.
- [Assessments](/docs/instructor/assessments) — proctored exam integrity:
  the review queue, flagged and terminated sessions.
- [Documents](/docs/instructor/documents) — lesson plans, report cards,
  worksheets and certificates, generated as real files.
- [Library](/docs/instructor/library) — what your agent has created, and
  your exam resources.
- [Money](/docs/instructor/money) — your wallet and AI credits, sales,
  earnings and payouts.
- [App Center](/docs/instructor/app-center) — platform tools for your
  agent, and exam resources to unlock.
- [Profile](/docs/instructor/profile) — your instructor profile and your
  agent's profile.
- [Settings](/docs/instructor/settings) — preferences, billing and
  account security.

# Home

Home is your dashboard — the first screen after you sign in. It answers
"what is happening today?" at a glance.

![The instructor dashboard in tablet view, with the icon rail on the left](/images/instructor/home/home.png)
_On a tablet the sidebar collapses to an icon rail; on a wide screen it shows full labels._

## The stat cards

Across the top:

- **Coin Balance** — your AI credits. Tap to top up; every AI feature
  draws from this balance.
- **Total Students** — learners enrolled across all your courses.
- **Active Courses** — courses currently live.
- **Total Courses** — published and draft courses together.
- **Revenue** — what you have earned from enrollments.
- **Agent XP** — your teaching agent's experience and level.
- **Streak** — your day streak. Keep it going.

If the figures cannot load, the dashboard says so — "We couldn't load
your stats, your profile, today's sessions. These figures may be
incomplete." — with a **Retry** button, instead of showing wrong numbers.

## Quick Actions

Six shortcuts to the jobs you do most:

- **New course** — build it in the Course Creator.
- **View Students** — manage your enrolled learners.
- **Add Student** — enroll a new learner.
- **Create Battle** — ask your agent to set one up.
- **Schedule a session** — add a live session to a course.
- **AI Assistant** — chat with your AI tutor.

## Your profile and agent cards

The card on the right shows you — your name, email, student count and
active courses. Below it, your **AI Agent** card shows your teaching
assistant's status, how many student questions it has answered
(**Responses**) and its **Satisfaction** rate. **Train Agent** takes you
to teaching your agent how you want it to answer.

## Today's Schedule

Your live sessions for today. When nothing is scheduled it says so, with
a **Schedule one** button that takes you to scheduling.

## Streaming Platforms

Connect the platforms you teach live on — **Zoom**, **Google Meet**,
**MS Teams** and **YouTube Live** each show **Not connected** until you
**Connect** them, or **Use link** to teach from a meeting link instead.

## The header

The top bar follows you everywhere: your coin balance, the **cart**,
**notifications**, and your agent's status — **Agent online** means your
agent is answering students; it can be switched off in
[Settings](/docs/instructor/settings).

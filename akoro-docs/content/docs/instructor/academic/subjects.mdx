# Subjects

**Subjects** lists the subjects taught at your school.

![Subjects](/images/instructor/academic/academic-subjects.png)
_Subjects taught at your school._

Each subject shows:

- its **name** — Mathematics, English Language, Biology, and the rest
  of your school's list;
- its **code** — the short form used across the school (MATHS, ENG,
  BIO…);
- its **type** — **core** or **elective**.

**New Subject** adds one. Subjects are the backbone of the Academic
pages: assignments to classes drive your
[Timetable](/docs/instructor/academic/timetables), outlines hang off
them in the [Syllabus](/docs/instructor/academic/curriculum-and-syllabus),
and the subjects you teach are declared on your
[Profile](/docs/instructor/profile).

# Timetables

Four views of the same school timetable, at four zoom levels.

## My Timetable

![My Timetable](/images/instructor/academic/academic-my-timetable.png)
_Your weekly teaching schedule._

**My Timetable** is your weekly teaching schedule — your classes, in
your week. It is built from the subjects your school has assigned you:
if you have none yet, the page says so — "You have no subjects
assigned yet. Ask your school to assign you to classes/subjects."

## Class Timetable

![The Class Timetable](/images/instructor/academic/academic-class-timetable.png)
_Printed weekly timetable per class._

The **Class Timetable** is the printed weekly timetable per class.
Pick a **class** and a section (or **All sections**) to view and edit
its timetable — "Pick a class to view and edit its timetable." The
Daily Schedule and Weekly Forecast both follow it.

## Daily Schedule

![The Daily Schedule](/images/instructor/academic/academic-daily-schedule.png)
_A single day's plan, period by period — follows the class timetable._

The **Daily Schedule** is a single day's plan, period by period. Pick
a **class** and a date, and the day fills in from the class timetable.

## Weekly Forecast

![The Weekly Forecast](/images/instructor/academic/academic-weekly-forecast.png)
_What will be taught each day — follows the class timetable._

The **Weekly Forecast** looks ahead: what will be taught each day of
the week. Pick a **class** and week to plan — it follows the class
timetable, so what you see is what the week will actually hold.

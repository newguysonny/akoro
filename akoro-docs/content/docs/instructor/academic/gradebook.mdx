# Gradebook

The **Gradebook** holds scores, weights and final grades for your
school classes.

![The Gradebook](/images/instructor/academic/academic-gradebook.png)
_Scores, weights and final grades._

## Pick a class

The class rail lists your classes with their sizes. **Pick a class** —
"Choose a class from the rail to see its gradebook." Until you do,
there is nothing to show: a gradebook is always one class at a time.

## Three ways to read it

- **Overall** — the class at a glance.
- **By Assignment** — how each assignment landed.
- **By Student** — each learner's standing.

And three products: **Assignments**, **Final Grades by Student** and
**Final Grades by Class**. **Add Assignment** records a new scored
piece of work directly.

## Weights and the grading scale

Scores become final grades through your **weights** and the **Grading
Scale** — the Grading System settings on this page decide how much
each assignment type counts and which score earns which grade. Change
them deliberately: final grades by student and by class recompute from
what you set here.

Grades you give in [Submissions](/docs/instructor/submissions) flow
into this gradebook automatically.

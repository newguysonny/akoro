# Curriculum and syllabus

## Curriculum

![Curriculum](/images/instructor/academic/academic-curriculum.png)
_Curriculum frameworks adopted by the school._

**Curriculum** lists the curriculum frameworks adopted by the school.
Filter by **Level** and **Status**, and switch between **Active** and
**Inactive** frameworks. **New Curriculum** adds one. A school that has
not adopted any yet shows **No records yet.**

## Syllabus

![Syllabus](/images/instructor/academic/academic-syllabus.png)
_Syllabus outline per subject._

**Syllabus** is the syllabus outline per subject. Filter by
**Subject**, **Class** and **Status** — again **Active** or
**Inactive** — and use **New Syllabus** to add an outline. The syllabus
is what your planning, your agent's lessons and the
[Weekly Forecast](/docs/instructor/academic/timetables) all teach
toward.

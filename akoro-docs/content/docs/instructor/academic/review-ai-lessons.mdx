# Review AI Lessons

The page's own title is **Review Generated Lessons**: approve or
correct AI-built lesson plans — students are never blocked while you
review.

![Review Generated Lessons](/images/instructor/academic/academic-review-ai-lessons.png)
_Approve or correct AI-built lesson plans — students are never blocked while you review._

## The three lists

- **Pending** — AI-built lesson plans waiting for your decision.
- **Approved** — plans you have passed.
- **Rejected** — plans you have sent back.

## How review works

Your agent builds lesson plans from your syllabus and your templates.
They queue here as **Pending**. Open one, read it the way a student
would meet it, and either approve it as it stands or correct it —
the plan your students get is the one you passed. Because students
are never blocked while you review, a plan still in the queue does
not stall the class; review is a quality gate, not a bottleneck.

What you correct teaches the agent your standard: adjust its
templates in [Documents](/docs/instructor/documents) and its defaults
in [Profile](/docs/instructor/profile), and the next plans arrive
closer to right.

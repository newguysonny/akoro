# Academic

**Academic** is the school side of your teaching. Where the rest of the
app is about your courses and your learners, these pages are about your
school: its classes, its timetable, its curriculum — and your place in
them.

Most Academic pages are **school-scoped**: they fill in once your
account is linked to your school and the school has assigned you
classes and subjects. If a page is empty, that is usually why — see
[Join your school](/docs/instructor/getting-started) in Getting
started.

## The pages

- [Timetables](/docs/instructor/academic/timetables) — **My
  Timetable** (your weekly teaching schedule), **Class Timetable**,
  **Daily Schedule** and **Weekly Forecast**.
- [Curriculum and syllabus](/docs/instructor/academic/curriculum-and-syllabus)
  — the frameworks your school has adopted, and the syllabus outline
  per subject.
- [Subjects](/docs/instructor/academic/subjects) — every subject taught
  at your school, with its code and type.
- [Gradebook](/docs/instructor/academic/gradebook) — scores, weights
  and final grades, by class, assignment or student.
- [Review AI Lessons](/docs/instructor/academic/review-ai-lessons) —
  approve or correct the lesson plans your agent builds.

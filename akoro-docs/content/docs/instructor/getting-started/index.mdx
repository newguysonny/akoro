# Getting started

The instructor app lives at **instructors.akoro.ng**. It is where you
build courses, run live classes, manage your students and get paid. This
portal is exclusively for instructors and administrators — learners use
the learner app instead.

## Create your account

Tap **Create an account** on the sign-in page, or go straight to the
sign-up page.

![The Become an instructor sign-up form](/images/instructor/getting-started/signup.png)
_Create your instructor account to publish courses and host live classes._

Fill in:

- **Full name** and **Username** — your username is how learners see you.
- **Email** — your sign-in email.
- **Password** — at least 8 characters.

Then tap **Create instructor account**. You can also use **Continue with
Google** to sign up with your Google account instead. By signing up you
agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy, and to teach in line
with Akoro's instructor guidelines.

## Sign in

![The Instructor Portal sign-in page](/images/instructor/getting-started/signin.png)
_Sign in to your instructor dashboard._

Enter your email and password and tap **Sign in**, or use **Continue
with Google**. If you cannot remember your password, tap **Forgot
password?** and a reset link will be sent to your email.

## Install the app

The instructor app is a web app you can install. When your browser offers
it, an **Install the Akoro instructor app** banner appears at the top of
your dashboard with an **Install** button — installing adds it to your
home screen, makes it faster, and lets it work offline.

## Join your school

If you teach at a school on Akoro, link your account to it from the
banner on your dashboard: pick your school under **Select school…** and
tap **Request to join**. Once your school approves, your classes and
students are linked to it, and the school-scoped pages under
[Academic](/docs/instructor/academic) — timetables, curriculum, syllabus —
fill in with your school's data.

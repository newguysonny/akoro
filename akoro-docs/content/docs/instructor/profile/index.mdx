# Profile

Profile manages your info and your AI agent, in two tabs: **Profile**
and **Agent**.

![Your instructor profile](/images/instructor/profile/profile.png)
_Manage your info and AI agent._

## Personal info

Your **Full Name**, **Username** (yours starts with @), **Email**,
**Bio** — "Tell students about yourself" — **Phone** and **Country**.
This is the you learners see; a complete profile is a trustworthy one.

## Teaching

- **School / Centre** — where you teach.
- **Institution Type**.
- **Subjects Taught** — the subjects you have added.

If you teach at a school on Akoro, also link your account from the
dashboard banner — see [Getting started](/docs/instructor/getting-started).

## Tool templates

Pick a default template per tool — **Lesson Plan**, **Worksheet**,
**Report** and **Exam / Assessment**. Each starts as **AI default (no
template)**: "Pick a default template per tool. If none is chosen, the
agent generates directly." The templates themselves live under
[Documents](/docs/instructor/documents).

## Overview

Your headline numbers: **Active Courses**, **Total Students**,
**Revenue** and **Agent XP**.

## The Agent tab

![The Agent tab of your profile](/images/instructor/profile/profile-agent.png)
_Your agent's profile._

The **Agent** tab is your teaching assistant's own profile — who it is
to your students and how it presents itself. Train and steer the agent
day to day in [chat](/docs/instructor/agent); its standing preferences
and tools are under [Settings](/docs/instructor/settings).

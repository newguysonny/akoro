# Your agent

Your agent is your AI teaching assistant. It answers your students'
questions around the clock in your style, and it works for you directly
in chat: drafting quizzes, preparing assignments, summarising submissions
and more. The Agent page has three tabs: **Chat**, **Tasks** and
**Stats**.

## Chat

![The agent chat, with template shortcuts and suggested prompts](/images/instructor/agent/agent-chat.png)
_Your agent, ready to work — templates on the left, suggestions above the composer._

The chat opens with suggestions you can tap instead of typing, such as:

- **Draft a quiz for my class**
- **Prepare an assignment**
- **How did the last assignment go?**
- **Plan this week's revision**
- **Summarise my submissions**

### Templates

Template shortcuts start the agent with a structured job:

- **Lesson Plan**, **Worksheets** and **Report Writing** — template fills
  that produce the document in your format.
- **Grading**, **Attendance / Roll Call**, **Interactive Material**,
  **Exam Prep Assessment**, **Quiz Generator**.
- **Student Progress**, **Send Reminder**, **Schedule Session**,
  **Reports & Battle Report**, **Create Battle**.

What your agent creates for you lands in the
[Library](/docs/instructor/library); documents it generates as files are
under [Documents](/docs/instructor/documents).

### How you pay for it

Agent work draws from your coin balance — the header shows it, and
[Wallet & Credits](/docs/instructor/money/wallet-and-credits) shows what
typical jobs cost. Your agent's own separate budget and behaviour are
managed in [Profile → Agent](/docs/instructor/profile) and
[Settings](/docs/instructor/settings).

## Tasks

![The Tasks tab](/images/instructor/agent/agent-tasks.png)
_Work you have given your agent, and its state._

The **Tasks** tab is where work you have delegated sits while the agent
carries it out, so a long job never traps you in the chat. The status at
the top of the Agent page — for example **Idle** — tells you whether
your agent is working on something right now.

## Stats

![The Stats tab](/images/instructor/agent/agent-stats.png)
_How your agent is doing with your students._

The **Stats** tab shows how your agent is performing for your students —
the same story as the agent card on [Home](/docs/instructor/home):
responses given and student satisfaction. If satisfaction dips, train
your agent from the Home card or Profile so its answers sound more like
you.

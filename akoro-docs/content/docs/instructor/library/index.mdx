# Library

Your Library holds what your agent has created, and your exam
resources. Everything the agent makes for you in chat is filed here so
you can find it again.

![The Library](/images/instructor/library/library.png)
_What your agent has created, and your exam resources._

## My resources

Your creations are grouped by kind: **Lesson Plans**, **Worksheets**,
**Reports**, **Assessments**, **Attendance**, **Interactive**,
**Podcasts**, **Lesson hooks**, **Misconceptions**, **Real-world**,
**Vocabulary**, **Activity packs** and **Assignment sheets** — with
**All** to see everything together.

When nothing is saved yet, the Library says so and suggests where to
start: "Ask your agent to create a lesson plan, podcast or hooks —
it'll appear here," with quick starts for a **Lesson plan**, a
**Podcast**, **Lesson hooks** and a **Vocabulary list**.

**Create in chat** takes you to [your agent](/docs/instructor/agent) to
make something new.

## Exam resources

![Exam resources](/images/instructor/library/library-exam.png)
_Your exam resources._

The **Exam resources** tab holds your examination materials — the past
papers and mark schemes you unlock in the
[App Center](/docs/instructor/app-center) appear here, ready to build
exam prep from.

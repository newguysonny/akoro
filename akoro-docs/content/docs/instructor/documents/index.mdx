# Documents

**Document Templates** are the paperwork of teaching, done properly:
lesson plans, report cards, worksheets and certificates — view them,
generate them as real files, and make them your own.

![Document Templates](/images/instructor/documents/documents.png)
_Lesson plans, report cards, worksheets and certificates — view and generate as real files._

## Browse by kind

The filter row groups templates by kind: **All**, **Form**,
**Certificate**, **Report**, **Result**, **Letter** and **Other**.

## Starter templates

Every account starts with a set of **STARTER** templates:

- **Lesson Plan** — a standard lesson plan with objectives and staged
  activities.
- **Report Card** — a termly student report card with subject scores
  and remarks.
- **Worksheet** — a classwork and practice worksheet with questions and
  answer lines.
- **Certificate of Achievement** — a landscape certificate with a
  decorative border and signatures.

## What you can do with a template

- **View** — see the template as it will come out.
- **Generate** — produce the real file, filled for the class or learner
  you choose.
- **Customize** — adjust the template itself so every future generation
  matches your format.

**Create Template** (also **New Template**) starts a template of your
own. Templates you pick as defaults for your agent's tools are set in
[Profile](/docs/instructor/profile) under Tool Templates.

# Akoro logo v2 (concept, not yet in the apps)

Transparent, logo only. An outlined "a" with three steps, white outline with a thin purple trace.

| File | Inside | Outline | Trace | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `akoro-logo-light.svg` | purple `#6C3BFF` | white | purple `#6C3BFF` | white |
| `akoro-logo-dark.svg` | dark `#0B0818` | white | purple `#7C55FF` | white |

The outline is one stroked path (28 wide, round caps), fitted to the original design so the curves
are smooth; the trace is the same path stroked 38 wide underneath. The steps are three rounded
polygons. `preview.png` shows both on their themes.

Not wired into any app yet. Current app logos are in `akoro-*/public/logo/`.

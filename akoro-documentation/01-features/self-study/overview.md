# Self-study overview

Instructor self-study authoring: activity drafts, generated question papers,
rubrics, reviews, grading and learner hand-in.

## What it covers

Instructors author self-study assignments from a brief (subject, topic,
subtopics, objectives). The platform generates a question plan, rubric and
paper; the instructor approves and assigns; learners hand in and are graded.

## Primary flow

1. Instructor creates an assignment from a brief.
2. A plan, questions and rubric are drafted, with versions and revision.
3. The instructor reviews, approves and publishes the assignment.
4. Learners start, answer with autosave, and hand in.
5. Grading supports teacher marks, AI drafts and instant provisional release.

## Status

Implemented and in use; treat as **beta** for public documentation.

## Permissions

- Instructor-owned drafts and assignments; ownership checks per route.
- Learner answers are per-learner and autosaved.

## Data

Assignments, drafts, routines, submissions and grades.

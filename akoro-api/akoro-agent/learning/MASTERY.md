# Mastery

- Mastery is a number between 0 and 1 that only the Education Engine moves,
  from recorded evidence.
- No evidence for a topic means unknown — not zero, not weak. Say "we have
  no record yet" rather than guessing.
- One passed diagnostic or retest settles a topic. Multiple attempts deepen
  evidence; they do not lower the bar.
- Gaps flagged during a session are recorded for the learner's teacher and
  for future sessions. Naming a gap honestly is a service, not a judgement.

# Teaching

Teach one topic at a time, and inside a topic, one idea at a time.

- Start from the learner's recorded weak points, not the textbook's order.
- A diagnostic comes before teaching: it shows what they actually know.
- After a below-threshold attempt, offer ONE strategy from the curated
  library — not a lecture. The learner chooses whether to use it.
- A teach-back is the learner explaining the topic in their own words. Your
  job is to listen and ask the next small question, not to grade them. The
  retest gate opens only when the learner confirms they have taught it back.
- Keep every explanation shorter than you think it needs to be.

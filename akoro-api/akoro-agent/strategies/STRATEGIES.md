# Strategy Library (v3)

The only strategies you may offer or choose. Each has its own guide
(strategies/<id>.md). The harness rejects anything else, anything switched
off, and anything not allowed in the current workflow (strategy_not_permitted).

Taught in a Teaching session:
- teach_back: the learner explains it to you; you probe. After a near-miss.
- different_analogy: a new everyday example for one fixed wrong picture.
- contrast_pairs: two easily-confused ideas side by side.
- vocabulary_focus: key terms first, with plain-language bridges.
- worked_examples: walk one, then the learner teaches you the next.
- varied_formats: the same idea as text, diagram, word problem.
- easy_wins_first: achievable items first, after confidence drops.
- make_concrete: real-life examples or a harder challenge when bored.
- check_your_work: a checking habit for careless slips.
- self_check: "How sure are you?" before a check, then compare.

Run by the harness (no teaching session):
- interleave: mix this topic with a related one (replan).
- space_out: come back to it later (schedule).
- prerequisite_review: the missing prerequisite first (replan).
- chunk_plan: smaller parts (replan).
- schedule_plan: a dated schedule to the exam (schedule).
- offline_pack: a downloadable pack for offline study (export).
- format_practice: practise the failing question format.
- goal_clarify: a measurable goal.
- peer_help: how others got past the same struggle.
- escalate_human: a person takes it up. Last resort.
- retrieval_practice, timed_practice: the learner answers (review and practice only).

Pick ONE strategy per offer, with a one-sentence reason grounded in the
learner's evidence. Never repeat a strategy that already failed on this topic.

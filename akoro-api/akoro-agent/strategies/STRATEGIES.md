# Strategy Library

The curated five — the only strategies you may offer:

- teach_back — the learner explains the topic in their own words; you listen
  and probe. Best after a near-miss.
- different_analogy — re-explain through a different everyday example. Best
  when the learner's answers show one fixed wrong mental model.
- interleave — mix this topic's practice with a related queued topic. Best
  for confusion between neighbouring concepts.
- space_out — schedule the topic for a later pass in this session. Best when
  fatigue, not understanding, is the problem.
- escalate_human — hand the gap to the learner's teacher or a human. Last
  resort, after the others have genuinely failed.

Pick ONE strategy per offer, with a one-sentence reason grounded in the
learner's evidence.

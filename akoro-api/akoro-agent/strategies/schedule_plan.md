# Strategy: schedule_plan

Runs in: the Study Prep harness (schedule).
Use for: no study plan, irregular practice, putting things off.

How it runs:
- The harness lays out dated sessions from now to the exam date, with reminders.
- Each session names its topic and roughly how long it takes.
- When you offer it, show the first two dates and ask if they suit the learner.

Success: the scheduled sessions are kept.
Avoid: a schedule denser than the learner's past pace.

# Strategy: self_check

Runs in: a Teaching session, or before checks in Study Prep.
Use for: not noticing being stuck, not acting on feedback, not asking for help.

How to run it:
- Before a check, ask "How sure are you?" (not sure, fairly sure, very sure).
- After it, compare the answer with the result, without judgement.
- Where they were sure and wrong, ask what they will do differently next time.

Success: the learner's confidence matches their results more closely.
Avoid: making the rating feel like a test of its own.

# Strategy: peer_help

Runs in: the harness brings peer stories into Teaching or chat.
Use for: any struggle that may be shared (never a sensitive one).

How it runs:
- The harness finds how other learners got past the same struggle (anonymous, at least five learners).
- Tell the learner, in one or two lines, what worked for others, and offer to try it together.
- Never name or describe another learner.

Success: the learner tries the peer's way and the next check improves.
Avoid: peer stories for confidence, anxiety, health or mindset struggles.

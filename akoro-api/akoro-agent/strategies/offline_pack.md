# Strategy: offline_pack

Runs in: the workflow harness (offline export): Study Prep or Decay Review.
Use for: device or connection problems, missing materials.

How it runs:
- The harness builds a downloadable pack: a short summary of each topic and practice questions with answers at the end.
- The learner studies offline; the next check happens when they are back online.
- When you offer it, say what is in the pack and how big it is.

Success: the pack is downloaded and the next check improves.
Avoid: packs larger than the learner's plan needs.

# Strategy: space_out

Runs in: the workflow harness (schedule): Study Prep, Decay Review or Practice.
Use for: forgetting over time, irregular practice, or a learner who is overloaded right now.

How it runs:
- The harness schedules a later pass on the topic (later in the session, or a next review date).
- Nothing is taught now; the topic is set aside, not dropped.
- When you offer it, say when it comes back and that tiredness, not ability, is the reason.

Success: the later pass is taken and the topic holds.
Avoid: using it to avoid a topic the learner keeps failing for a reason other than fatigue.

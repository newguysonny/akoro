# Strategy: escalate_human

Runs in: the workflow harness (notify a human): the learner's instructor, or a person who can help.
Use for: frustration or giving up, a learner who will not ask for help, or any struggle after other strategies have genuinely failed.

How it runs:
- The harness notifies the learner's instructor (or asks a human) with the topic and what was tried.
- The learner is told plainly that a person will look at it, and what happens meanwhile.
- Share only what the instructor needs: the topic, the struggle type and the strategies already tried.

Success: a person picks it up.
Avoid: escalating first; escalating more than once per topic.

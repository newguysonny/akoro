# Strategy: make_concrete

Runs in: a Teaching session.
Use for: a learner who has switched off: short, off-topic or bored replies.

How to run it:
- Tie the idea to something real in the learner's day (cooking, transport, a phone, a farm).
- Or raise the challenge: a puzzle or a "what would happen if" question.
- Ask the learner for their own real example.

Success: the learner engages again, with longer, on-topic replies.
Avoid: jokes at the learner's expense; examples from a world they do not know.
